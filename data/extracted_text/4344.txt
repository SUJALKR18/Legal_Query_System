An Act further to amend the Assam Taxation (Liquidation of Arrear Dues) Act, 2005.
Preamble:- Whereas it is expedient further to amend the Assam Taxation (Liquidation of Arrear Dues) Act, 2005,
hereinafter referred to as the principal Act, in the manner hereinafter appearing;
It is hereby enacted in the Seventieth Year of the Republic of India as follows: -
1.
Short title, extent and commencement:-
(1)
This Act may be called the Assam Taxation (Liquidation of Arrear Dues) Act, 2019.
(2)
It shall have the like extent as the principal Act.
