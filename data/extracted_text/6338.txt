Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 1949
An Act to amend the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937 (Central Act XXVI of 1937), in its application
to the [ State of Tamil Nadu]
[Substituted for the expression 'Province of Madras' by paragraph 3(2) of the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order,
1970.]
.
Whereas it is expedient to amend the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application Act, 1937 (Central Act XXVI of 1937),
in its application to the [State of Tamil Nadu]
[Substituted for the expression 'Province of Madras' by paragraph 3(2) of the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order,
1970.]
in the manner hereinafter appearing; It is hereby enacted as follows :-
1.
Short title.
- This Act may be called the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) Application [Tamil Nadu]

Adaptation of Laws (Second Amendment) Order, 1969.]
Amendment) Act 1949.
This Act was extended to the Kanyakumari district and the Shencottah taluk of the Tirunel-veli district by section 3 of,
and the First Schedule to, the Tamil Nadu (Transferred Territory) Extension of Laws Act, 1960 (Tamil Nadu Act 23 of
1960).

- The Muslim Personal Law (Shariat) [Application]

3.
Application of Personal Law to Muslims.

2. []

1957 (Tamil Nadu Act XXV of 1957). In so far as this Act applies to the added territories, this figure was inserted by

Notwithstanding any custom or usage to the contrary, in all questions regarding intestate succession, special property of
females, including personal property inherited or obtained under contract or gift or any other provision of personal law,
marriage, dissolution of marriage, including Tallaq, Ila, Zihar, Lian, Khula and Mubaraat, maintenance, dower,
guardianship, gifts, trusts and trust properties and wakfs the rule of decision in cases where the parties are Muslims
shall be the Muslim Personal Law (Shariat).".
