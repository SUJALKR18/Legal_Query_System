The M.P. Ceiling On Agricultural Holdings Act, 1960
(No. 20 of 1960)
Statement of Objects and Reasons. - With a view to provide for a more equitable distribution of land, it is considered
expedient to fix ceilings on existing holdings as well as on future acquisition of Agricultural lands. The surplus land
vesting in Government will be allotted on payment of occupancy price to needy persons and co-operative farming
societies in certain priorities. The Bill is, therefore, being introduced to promote the economic and social interests of the
weaker of the community and to subserve the common good.
[Dated 20th September, 1960]
Received the assent of the President on the 20th September, 1960; assent first published in the "Madhya Pradesh
Gazette" Extraordinary, on the 1st October, 1960.
