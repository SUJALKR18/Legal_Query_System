Short title and commencement.
(1)
These Regulations may be called the Uttar Pradesh State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Integration and
Seniority) (Sixth Amendment) Regulations, 1985.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from May 28, 1985.
2.
Substitution of Regulation.
- In the Uttar Pradesh State Electricity Board Service of Engineers (Integration and Seniority) Regulations, 1976 for
Regulation 6 (2) the following regulation shall be substituted -
"Seniority of Engineers from Ex-licensee Companies

(2)

Chairman;
(ii)
one wholetime member of the Board nominated by the Chairman; and
(iii)
Law Officer of the Board."
