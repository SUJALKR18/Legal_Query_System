The Indian Rifles (Repeal) Act, 2005
Act No. 49 of 2006
[17th December, 2006.]
An Act to repeal the Indian Rifles Act, 1920.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:--
1.
Short title.?
This Act may be called the Indian Rifles (Repeal) Act, 2006.
