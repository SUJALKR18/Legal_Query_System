Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Furnishing and Maintenance of Residence) Rules, 2006
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R.71, dated 27.1.2007
Last Updated 21st May, 2019
G.S.R.71. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 11 read with sub-Sections 1(a) and (2) of Section 6-A of the
Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act
No. 6 of 1957
), the State Government hereby makes are following rules, namely:

1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Furnishing and Maintenance of Residence)
Rules, 2006.
(2)
They shall come into force at once.
2.
Definitions.
- In these rules, unless the subject or context otherwise requires;
(a)
"Act" means the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956
(Rajasthan Act
No. 6 of 1957
);
(b)
"Official residence" means a building set apart or allotted by the Government for the residence of the said officers; and
(c)
Words and expression used but not defined in these rules, shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in the Act.
3.
Applicability.
- These rules shall apply to the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly, the Government
Chief Whip, the Deputy Government Chief Whip and the Leader of the Opposition.
4.
Furniture facilities and maintenance.
- When a building is earmarked or allotted as an official residence of the Speaker, the Deputy Speaker, the Government
Chief Whip, the Deputy Government Chief Whip and the Leader of the Opposition, the maximum expenditure to be
incurred on the initial furnishing thereof shall be limited to the amount equivalent to the expenditure permissible for a
minister's official residence under the Rajasthan Ministers Official Residences (Use and Maintenance) Rules, 1966, as
amendment from time to time.
5.
Repeal.
- The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Speaker's (Furnishing and Maintenance of Residence) Rules, 1990 is hereby
repealed.
