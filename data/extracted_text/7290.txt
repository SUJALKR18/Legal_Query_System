), read with sub-section (3) of Section 39 of the said Adhiniyam, the Governor is pleased to make the U. P. 2 [Zila
Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Terms and Conditions for Holding of Certain Posts by Government Officers) Rules, 1968, after previous publication with
Notification No. 7182-B/33-2-125-62, dated July 24, 1967, as required under sub-section (2) of Section 237 of the said
Adhiniyam :
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These Rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Terms and Conditions for Holding of Certain Posts by Government Officers) Rules, 1968.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Gazette.
2.
Section 39 (3).
- The Additional District Magistrate (Planning) or the District Planning Officer, as the case may be, the District Medical
Officer of Health, the Deputy Inspector of Schools, the District Panchayat Raj Officer, the Senior Mechanical Inspector
(Minor Irrigation), the District Livestock Officer, the District Agriculture Officer and the Assistant Registrar, Co-operative
Societies exercising jurisdiction in the district or the District Co-operative Officer, as the case may be, of the State
Government shall hold respectively the posts of Mukhya Adhikari, Swasthya Adhikari, Shiksha Adhikari, Panchayat Raj
Adhikari, Sinchai Adhikari, Pashudhan Adhikari, Krishi Adhikari and Sahkarita Adhikari, under the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
on the following terms and conditions :
(i)
The appointing authority in respect of the aforesaid officers shall continue to be the same as before.
(ii)
The entire expenditure on account of pay, allowances and pensions, etc. shall continue to be borne by the State
Government.
(iii)
The power to dismiss, remove from service or reduce in rank or to a lower stage in time-scale or stop increments shall
continue to vest in the respective Government authorities, who enjoy that power over these officers as Government
servants.
(iv)
The power to inflict minor punishment such as warning and censure shall vest in the Government authority as hitherto.
(v)
The transfer of these officers from one district to another will continue to be made by the respective Government
authorities.
(vi)
The power to sanction any leave, other than the casual leave to the Mukhya Adhikari and other officers shall vest in the
State Government authorities concerned as hitherto.
