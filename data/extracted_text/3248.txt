Coinage of One Hundred Rupees coin to commemorate the occasion of Birth Centenary of Smt. ...
(Department of Economic Affairs)
G.S.R. 405(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of section 24 of the
Coinage Act, 2011(11 of 2011), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely: -
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Coinage of One Hundred Rupees coin to commemorate the occasion of Birth Centenary
of Smt. Vijaya Raje Scindia Rules, 2019.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Denomination of coin.
- The coin of One Hundred denomination shall also be coined at the Mint for issue under the authority of the Central
Government on the occasion of "Birth Centenary of Smt. Vijaya Raje Scindia".
3.
Dimension and composition of coin.
- The coin of the denomination mentioned in rule 2 shall conform to the following dimensions and composition, namely: -

of Smt. Vijaya Raje Scindia and the remedy allowed in making of such coin shall be as specified in the Second Schedule
