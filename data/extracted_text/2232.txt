The Spirituous Preparations (Inter-State Trade And Commerce) Control (Repeal) Act, 2006
NO. 32 of 2006
1133.
[22nd August, 2006]
An Act to repeal the Spirituous Preparations (Inter-State Trade and Commerce) Control Act, 1955. Be it enacted by
Parliament in the Fifty-seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Spirituous Preparations (Inter-State Trade and Commerce) Control (Repeal) Act, 2006.
