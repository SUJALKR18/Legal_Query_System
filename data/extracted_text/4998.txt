THE BIHAR APPROPRIATION ACT, 2020
Act 2 of 2020
Published in Bihar Gazette on 5 March 2020
Commenced on 1 April 2019
[This is the version of this document from 5 March 2020.]
AN ACT
TO AUTHORISE PAYMENT AND APPROPRIATION OF CERTAIN AMOUNT FROM AND OUT OF THE
CONSOLIDATED FUND OF THE STATE OF BIHAR FOR THE SERVICES DURING THE YEAR ENDING THE
THIRTYFIRST DAY OF MARCH, 2020.
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Bihar in the seventy-first year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title, extent and commencement .-
(1)
This Act may be called the Bihar Appropriation Act, 2020.
(2)
It shall extend to the whole of the state of Bihar.
(3)

It shall come intoforce with effect from 1st April 2019.
2.
Withdrawal of total Rs.- 8868,52,58,000 (Eight thousand Eight hundred Sixty eight crore Fifty two lakh and Fifty eight
Thousand), out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Bihar for the year 2019-20.- Total Rs.- 8868,52,58,000 (Eight
thousand Eight hundred Sixty eight crore Fifty two lakh and Fifty eight Thousand), which will not be more than the
amount mentioned in column-6 of the schedule of this Act from The Consolidated Fund of The State of Bihar to meet
various expenses to be incurred related to the payment during the year commencing from 1st April, 2019 in relation to
the services mentioned in column-2 of the Schedule of this Act.
3.
Appropriation.- The amount authorized for withdrawal from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Bihar under
this Act will be for the purposes and services mentioned in the Schedule concerned related to the year commencing
from first day of April, 2019 and will be appropriated in respect of them.
