The Constitution (Distribution of Revenues) No.7 Order, 2009
In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (2) of Article 275 of the Constitution, the President, after having
considered the recommendations of the Twelfth Finance Commission, hereby makes the following Order, namely :-
1.
This Order may be called the Constitution (Distribution of Revenues) No.7 Order, 2009.
