U.P. Zila Panchayats (Heads of Departments) Rules, 1968
Published vide Notification No. 2481-B/33-2-1-R-66, dated 18th April, 1968
In exercise of the powers wider Section 237 of me Uttar Pradesh [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U. P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U. P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
Adhiniyam, 1961 (U. P. Act No. 33 of 1961), read with sub-section (4) of Section 39 of the said Adhiniyam, the Governor
is pleased to make the following Uttar Pradesh [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U. P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Heads of Departments) Rules, 1968, after previous publication with Notification No. 3010-B/33-2-I-R-66, dated July 22,
1967, as required under sub-section (2) of Section 237 of the said Adhiniyam :

U.P. Zila Panchayats (Heads of Departments) Rules, 1968
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These Rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U. P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Heads of Departments) Rules, 1968.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Gazette.
