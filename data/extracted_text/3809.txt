, Printed at 47 (A.P. Acts).]
, (Andhra Pradesh Ordinance 6 of 2002) the Governor of Andhra Pradesh hereby makes the following rules. Prescribing
time limits for speedy processing and disposal of applications by the competent authority and notification of clearances
where the provisions of deemed approval shall apply.
1.
Short Title, Extent and Commencement.
- (i) These rules may be called 'The Andhra Pradesh Industrial Single Window Clearance (Time Limit and deemed
approval) Rules, 2002".
(ii)
They shall extend to the whole of the State of Andhra Pradesh these rules shall come into force with effect from the date
of issue of notification in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette.
2.
Time limits for processing and disposal of applications.
- The time limits for processing and disposal of applications by the competent authority under the provisions of
sub-section (2) of Section 14 of the said Ordinance shall be as indicated in the Schedule-I.
3.
Notifying of clearances where the provision of deemed approval shall apply.
- The provisions of deemed approval under sub-section (1) of Section 15 of the said Ordinance shall be applicable to the
clearances as indicated in Schedule-II, in case the competent authority fails to pass final orders on the application within
the time limit stipulated as indicated in Schedule-I.
