The Colonial Prisoners Removal (Repeal) Act, 2001
ACT NO. 12 OF 2001
1176.
[9th May, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Colonial Prisoners Removal Act, 1884.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Colonial Prisoners Removal (Repeal) Act, 2001.
31.
2. Repeal of Acts 47 and 48 Vict., C. 31.
