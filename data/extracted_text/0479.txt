lieu of drawing travelling allowance under the ordinary rules governing a journey on transfer applicable to him, travel in a
reserved compartment of the highest class, including air conditioned.
(2)

no accommodation had been reserved, and must, in addition, pay in cash to the station master of the station from which
the journey commences, the fares for any members of his family accompanying him whether they share his reserved

8.
Repeal and Saving.
(1)
The High Court Judges (Part - A States) Travelling Allowance Rules, 1950, are hereby repealed.
