The U.P. Goshala Rules, 1964
Published vide Notification No. 8115/12-E-144-63, dated January 16, 1965, published in the U.P. Gazette, Part 1-A,
dated January 30, 1965
In exercise of powers under Section 20 of Uttar Pradesh Goshala Adhiniyam, 1964 (U.P. Act No. X of 1964), the
Governor of Uttar Pradesh is pleased to make the following Rules:
1.
Short title and commencement - [Section 20].
(1)
These rules may be called Uttar Pradesh Goshala Rules, 1964.
(2)
They shall apply to the areas to which provisions of the Uttar Pradesh Goshala Adhiniyam, 1964 (U.P. Act X of 1964),

are applied under sub-section (3) of Section 1 of the said Act.
2.
Definitions.
- In these rules unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context:
(a)
"Adhiniyam" means the Uttar Pradesh Goshala Adhiniyam, 1964;
(b)
"Form" means a form appended to these rules;
(c)
"Section" means section of the Adhiniyam;
(d)
"representative" means a person elected or nominated by the trustees of a Goshala under Section 3.
3.
Federation and its constitution - [Section 3 (2) and Section 20 (2) (a)].
(1)
The Federation shall consist of thirty members including the President, a Vice-President, a Secretary, a Joint Secretary,
and a Treasurer.
(2)
The members of the Federation shall be elected by the representatives of the Goshala of the State from amongst
themselves in the following manner, namely:
(i)
within three months of the preparation of the Pradesh Goshala Register a notice specifying the date, place and time of
the meeting for election of the members and office-bearers of the Federation shall be sent by the Registrar under postal
certificate to each trustee of the Goshala at least twenty-one days before the date of the meeting;
(ii)
the Director of Animal Husbandry or in his absence the Registrar shall preside over the meeting for the election of
members and office-bearers of the Federation;
(iii)
the quorum for the meeting shall consist of three-fourth of the total number of representatives:
Provided that if at any meeting the quorum is not complete, the Registrar or the President, as the case may be, may
adjourn the meeting to some other date and the notice of such meeting shall be issued in the same manner as is
prescribed to clause (i) and no quorum shall be required for an adjourned meeting;
(iv)
all votes shall be recorded by the representatives in person in such manner as may be determined by the presiding
officer; and
(v)
the election of each member or officer-bearer shall be by a majority of votes and in the case of an equality of votes the
presiding officer shall have a casting vote.
(3)
The President, office-bearers and other members of the Federation shall hold office for a period of three years :
Provided that the term of a person elected as a member or office bearer to fill a casual vacancy shall be for the
unexpired term of the member or office bearer as the case may be, in whose place he has been elected.
(4)
(i)
The election of the President, office-bearers and other members of the Federation shall be completed as far as possible
within two months after the expiry of the term of three years.
(ii)
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained elsewhere in these rules, the term of the Federation may be
extended by the Registrar for any period till its reconstitution.
4.
Procedure for the conduct of other business.

- Subject to the provisions contained in these rules and the Adhiniyam, the Federation may determine the procedure for
the conduct of its business.
5.
Proceedings to be recorded.
- Proceedings of the meeting of the Federation shall be recorded in Minute Book signed by the person for the time being
presiding over the meeting and the Secretary of the Federation.
6.
Submission of statement by trustee - [Section 4].
(1)
The trustee of every Goshala shall submit to the Registrar the statement required under sub-section (1) of Section 4 in
Form I which shall be signed by the trustee or his authorised agent and verified by the person signing the statement in
the manner prescribed in respect of a plaint in a civil suit.
(2)
Where any statement required to be delivered to the Registrar under the Adhiniyam for registration is found to be
incomplete or defective in any of the particulars required by Adhiniyam, the Registrar shall return it for rectification and
shall not register or file it until all the requirements of the Adhiniyam have been complied with. When the Registrar is
satisfied that provisions prescribed in respect thereof have been duly complied with he shall record an entry of the
statement in the Pradesh Goshala Register and shall file the statement.
7.
Maintenance of register and issue of certificate - [Section 6 (1)].
- The Pradesh Goshala Register maintained by the Registrar under Section 6 shall be in Form II and the Registrar shall
grant a certificate of registration in Form III to the trustee or his agent.
8.
Section 6 (2).
- The copies or extracts of the Pradesh Goshala Register shall be furnished by the Registrar on receipt of an application
along with a fee of rupee one only. The fee shall either the deposited in cash in the office of the Registrar or by postal
order.
9.
Change in particulars of Goshala to be reported - [Section 7 (2)].
- The manner or signing and verifying a report under Section 7 shall be the same that of plaint in a civil suit.
10.
Notice of enquiry - [Section 8 (2)].
- The notice of enquiry under sub-section (2) of Section 8 to be served on the person shall be in Form IV. The notice
shall also be published in one issue of both English and Hindi newspapers having large circulation in the areas
concerned.
11.
Issue of notice of demand for failure to pay fees - [Section 9].
- On the failure of a trustee to pay the fee required under Section 9 the Registrar shall issue a notice of demand to the
trustee in Form V.
12.
[Section 10 (2)].
- The accounts of receipt and expenditure shall be maintained by the Goshala in Form VI.
13.
The accounts maintained by the trustee of a Goshala shall be examined and audited annually after the close of the year
on 31st day of March by a firm of Chartered Accountants.
14.
Statement of accounts to be furnished by trustee - [Section 10 (5)].
- The trustee of every Goshala shall submit to the Registrar a statement of account required under sub-section (5) of
Section 10, in Form VII.
15.
[Section 11].

(1)
After fixing the area of a Goshala on the recommendation of the Federation under sub-section (1) of Section 11, the
Director shall send a notice in Form VIII to the trustee of a Goshala and a copy of that notice to the Registrar.
(2)
All realizations made by the traders and merchants under sub-section (3) of Section 11 shall be paid within thirty days of

(3)
A merchant or trader paying money in accordance with sub-section (3) of Section 11 aforesaid shall be entitled to get
from the Goshala of the area concerned collection expenses, at the rate of 20 per cent.
16.
[Section 12].
- Each trader and merchant shall furnish to the District Livestock Officer, of the district concerned half-yearly statements
of realization made in Form No. IX within thirty days of the expiry of each half year.
17.
Issue of notice by the Registrar [Section 13 (1)].
- On the application of a trustee of Goshala under sub-section (1) of Section 13 against a merchant or a trader, the
Registrar may for the purpose of ascertaining the moneys payable by the trader to the trustee, issue a notice in Form X.
