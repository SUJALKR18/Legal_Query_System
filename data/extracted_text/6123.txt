The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Concession on Account of Electricity and Water) Rules, 1981
Published vide Notification No. F.7(8) Sansad/81, dated 22-10-1981, Published in Rajasthan Gazette Part 4-C Page 229
(6-7) dated 22-10-1981
G.S.R. 71. - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 read with proviso to Sub-section (4) of section 6A of the
Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act
6 of 1957
), the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:-

The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Concession on Account of Electricity and Wate...
1.
Short title.
- These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Concessions on account of Electricity and
Water) Rules, 1981.
2.
Applicability.
- These rules shall apply to the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and also to the
Government Chief Whip, Deputy Government Chief Whip and Leader of the Opposition who are hereinafter referred to
as the said officers.
3.
Concession on account of electricity & water.
- For purposes of Sub-section (4) of section 6A of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members
Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act
6 of 1957
) the said officers shall be entitled to the concession of payment by Government for him and on his behalf throughout the
term of his behalf throughout the term of his office and upto a period of two months from the date he ceases to hold such
office of all charges due from him on account of the consumption of electricity and water at his residence subject to the
condition that concession of such payment shall not exceed the limit of 13000 units of electricity and 35 lakhs litres of
water per annum.
4.
Repeal.
- The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Concessions on account of Electricity and Water) Rules, 1965 are
hereby repealed.
