Rajasthan Video Films (Regulation of Exhibition) Rules, 1992
Published vide Notification No. F.10 (34)FD/Gr.4/90-2 (GSR 9), 1.6.1992 Published in Rajasthan Gazette Extraordinary
4(Ga)(I), dated 1.6.1992  page 35
Notification No. F.10 (34)FD/Gr.4/90-2 (GSR 9), 1.6.1992 - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 14 of the
Rajasthan Video Films (Regulation of Exhibition)Act, 1990 (Act
No. 11 of 1991
), the State Government hereby makes the following Rules for the purpose of carrying out all or any of the provisions of
the said Act, namely :-
1.
Short title and commencement
(1)

----- PAGE 2 -----
Rajasthan Video Films (Regulation of Exhibition) Rules, 1992
These rules shall be called the Rajasthan Video films (Regulation of Exhibition) Rules, 1992.
(2)
They shall apply to exhibition by means of video in a place in Rajasthan.
(3)
They shall come into force at once.
2.
Definitions
- (A) "Act" means the Rajasthan Video Films (Regulation of Exhibition) Act, 1990 :
(b)
"Form" means a form appended to these rules :
(c)
"Section" means a section of the Act;
(d)
"Licensing Authority" means authority as defined under the Rajasthan video films (Regulation of Exhibition) Act. 1990 :
(e)
"Licence" means a written authorisation by the Licensing Authority to hold exhibition by means of video granted in Form
V-2 or Form V-3 as the case may be, appended to these rules;
(f)
"Licensee" means a person who has been granted a Licence and includes his agent appointed for the purpose under a
power of attorney and whose appointment has been intimated in writing to the licensing authority;
(g)
"Permanent building" means .a building which is constructed for permanent use with stone, mud, brick, mortar, cement
or other non-inflammable material;
(h)
"Public Service vehicle" means a vehicle as defined in the motor Vehicle Act 1988.
3.
Restriction on the grant of Licence
- No person shall be granted licence unless the Licensing Authority is satisfied that the provisions of the Act and these
rules have been complied with.
4.
Requirements of permanent building, seating, etc. for Video Cinema
(1)
There shall be an independent entrance on the public thoroughfare on which the site of such building abuts.
(2)
The building shall be provided with open space of not less than 3 metres in width on any two sides, and open space of
not less than 6 metres in width in the front for parking of vehicles. If the building is away from thoroughfare, the approach
road shall not be less than 3 metres in width.
(3)
The building :
(a)
shall be well built, structurally safe and constructed of non inflammable material :
(b)
shall be sufficiently ventilated :
(c)
shall have 1.6 metres wide verandah on any one side of the door-ways of the building;
(d)
shall have at least one doorway per 50 seats and in any case not less than two: fitted with doors to open outwards and
the size of the doors shall not be less than 1.95 metres in height and 1.34 metres in width;
(e)
shall have normal height of the roof or its immediate covering from the floor not less than 3.5 metres with electric
installations fitted at a height of 2.75 metres; and

(4)
In case the auditorium is constructed on the first floor or the second floor of a building, it shall have 1.6 metres wide

5.
Requirements of a permanent building for Video Parlour
(a)
The building shall be well built structurally safe and constructed of non-inflammable material; and (b) shall be sufficiently
ventilated.
6.
Fee.
- The annual fee for grant or renewal of licence shall be Rs. 200/-.
7.
Display of Licence.
- The Licensee shall display his licence at a conspicuous place where exhibition by means of video is held.
8.
Provision for Fire Extinguisher.
- One portable fire extinguisher of C.T.C. and one Soda Acid of 1 Kg. capacity and one bucket (9 Litres) filled with sand
and two buckets with water shall be 'provided in the premises of video cinema/parlour.
9.
Application for Licence.
(1)
The application for licence for holding exhibition by means of video shall be submitted in Form V-1 to the licensing
authority along with the documents mentioned in sub-rule (2) or (3) , as the case may be.
(2)
In the case of permanent building, including hotel and restaurant the application shall be accompanied with the following
documents :-
(a)
Attested photostat copy of licence obtained for commercial use of video or television under the Indian Telegraph Act,
1885 (Act 13 of 1885) :
(b)
A site plan in duplicate showing location of the premises in relation to adjacent premises within a radius of 100 metres
public thorough fare on which the building abuts including the place marked for parking of vehicles drawn on the scale of
1:5; and
(c)
A building plan in duplicate including longitudinal and latitudinal sections and seating plan containing the ring the
arrangements of seats in each class including gang way and places left open, drawn on the scale of 1:1
The documents referred in clause (b) and (c) shall be required only in case of video cinemas.
(3)
In the case of public service vehicle, the application shall be accompanied with the documents referred to in clause (a)
of sub-rule (2) and an attested photo copy of document of its registration under Motor Vehicle Act denoting therein the
route, if any of the operation of the said vehicle.
10.
Deposit of Fee.
- The licence fee and any other fee be deposited into the Government Accounted under the head "0045-Other Taxes
and Duties on Commodities & Service".

11.
Other conditions for grant of licence.
- Licence shall not be granted for a video cinema, if it is situated:
(i)
Within a radius of 500 metres of a permanent cinema either existing or under construction in a town with a population
exceeding 5 lakhs;
(ii)
Within a radius of 1000 metres of a permanent cinema either existing or under construction in a town with a population
exceeding 50,000 but not exceeding 5 lakhs; and
(iii)
Within a radius of 1500 metres of a permanent cinema either existing or under construction in a town with a population
not exceeding 50,000.
Explanation:- (a) For the purposes of this rule, the population of 1991 census shall be taken into account while granting
licence.
(b)
The already holding exhibition by means of video in a permanent building before the commencement of these rules.
12.
Issue of Licence
- The licensing authority may on receipt of application under sub-section (1) of Section 4 of the Act, after having satisfied
that all the requirements have been fulfilled, shall issue the licence in form V-2 or V-3, as the case may be, appended to
these rules.
13.
Refusal of Licence
- The licensing authority may for reasons to be recorded in writing, refuse a licence if he is satisfied that the exhibition by
means of video in a video cinema or video parlour or hotel or restaurant is likely to cause obstruction, inconvenience,
annoyance, risk, danger or damage to the residents or passer-by in the vicinity of the place of exhibition.
14.
Relaxation in respect of permanent building
(1)
Any person already holding exhibition by means of video. Cinema in a permanent building before the date of
commencement of these Rules may be granted relaxation by the licensing authority from any of the provisions of rule 4
if the licensing authority is satisfied that the public safety, security and convenience is no jeopardized by such relaxation.
(2)
Exhibition of films in case of video parlour may be permitted by the licensing authority in identifiable open enclosures in
special circumstance for such period and with such conditions as laid down in the licence.
15.
Levy of entertainment Tax
- The entertainment tax on payment for admission to video cinema/parlour shall be governed by the provisions in rule
15A of the Rajasthan Entertainments & Advertisements Tax Rules, 1957.
