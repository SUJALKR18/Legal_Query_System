Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Ex-Members (Free Transit by Road Transport Service) Rules, 1989
Published vide Notification No. F. 7(1) Sansad/89, dated 1.7.1989
No. F. 7(1) Sansad/89. - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 read with sub-section (2) of section 4-A of the
Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act
No. 6 of 1957
), the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely : -
1.

Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Ex-Members (Free Transit by Road Transport Service) Rules, ...
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly [Ex-Members]
[Notification F7(1)sansad/89 Jaipur dated Dec. 17, 2009 w.e.f. at once.]
(Free Transit by Road Transport Service) Rules, 1989.
(2)
They shall be deemed to have come into force on 1st day January, 1989.
2.
Definition.
- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires: -
(a)
"Act" means the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956;
(b)
"Pass" means the "Identity-Cum-Road Journey Pass" issued under rule 3;
(c)
["Ex-Member"]
[Notification No. F7 (17) Sansad/1998 Jaipur, dated June 18, 2008. w.e.f. April 1, 2008.]
means a person who is a recipient of or is entitled to pension under section 4-A of the Act;
(d)
"Secretary" means the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and includes any officer of the said Assembly and
specially empowered by the Speaker to perform the functions of the Secretary under these rules;
(e)
"Service Bus" includes all kind of buses run by Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation or buses run on contract
basis;
(f)
"State Roadways" means the Rajasthan State Road Transport Corporation Service.
3.
[Ex-Member entitled to travel on passes.
[Notification No. F7 (17) Sansad/1998 Jaipur, dated June 18, 2008. w.e.f. April 1, 2008.]
- Subject to the provisions of these rules, every [Ex-member] shall be provided by the Secretary with Two free
non-transferable passes in the form given in Appendix-AA which shall entitle him and any other person accompanying
him to travel at any time free of charge by the service buses of the State Roadways and on any route on which the State
Roadways operate.]
4.
[Ex-member entitled to travel on passes.
[Notification No. F7 (1) Sansad/2010 Jaipur, dated 9.11.10 w.e.f. 1.4.2010.]
- Be each year, every ex-members shall be supplied by the secretary Four books containing [ex-member] and his
attendant's Bus Journey Form's with the foils in Appendix-BB. Each book shall contain 50 serial numbers and until one
book is exhausted the ex-member shall not use the another book and at the end of every year the ex-member shall
return the books to the Secretary and until the books are returned as aforesaid the Secretary shall not issue new book
for the next year.]
5.
Procedure for journey.
(1)
On the commencement of a journey the [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7 (1) Sansad/89 Jaipur, dated Dec 17, 2009 w.e.f. at once.]
shall fill in the form and the foils thereof and hand over the form to the conductor of the bus. The conductor shall thereon
fill in the amount of fare chargeable for the journey on the foil with the Ex-member. A separate form shall be used for
each journey by different buses.
(2)
An [Ex-member]

(3)
An [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7 (1) Sansad/89 Jaipur, dated Dec 17, 2009 w.e.f. at once]
wishing to reserve his seat may do so on payment of charges for reservation.
6.
Permissible luggage.
- [An Ex-member shall be entitled to carry such luggage free of charge as is permissible to a passenger for the class in
which the Ex-member travels and [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7 (1) Sansad/89 Jaipur, dated Dec 17, 2009 w.e.f. at once.]
shall have to pay for the excess luggage, if any, on the rates fixed in that behalf by the State Roadways.]
7.
Period of validity of the pass.
- The pass granted under these rules shall be valid only during the period the holder continues to be an [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7(1) Sansad/89 dated 17.12.2009.]
under the provisions of section 4-A of the Act and the holder shall return the pass to the Secretary as soon as he ceases
to be an [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7(1) Sansad/89 dated 17.12.2009.]
.
8.
[ Report of loss of pass, etc.
[Substituted by Rajasthan Notification No. G.S.R. 112, dated 17.11.2015 (w.e.f. 1.7.1989).]
- When an Ex-member loses his Pass/Free Journey Form Book, he shall immediately report the fact of loss to the
Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and Executive Director (Traffic) Rajasthan State Roadways Transport
Corporation. On receipt of such report, a fresh Pass/Free Journey Form book, as the case may be, may be issued by
the Secretary to the Ex-member on his paying rupees fifty in the case of the Pass and rupees fifty in the case of each
Free Journey form book. If the Pass/free Journey form book is subsequently found. it shall be surrendered to the
secretary.]
9.
Provision for payment of amount due on account of the journey.
- The Secretary shall arrange to make the payment from the budget of the Legislative Assembly the amount due on
account of the journey undertaken by the [Ex-member]
[Notification No. F7(1) Sansad/89 dated 17.12.2009.]
, as determined on the basis of the [Ex-member's]
[Notification No. F7(1) Sansad/89 dated 17.12.2009.]
Bus Journey Forms (duplicate foils) sent by the State Roadways for payment.
