The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Advance for Purchase of Motor Car) Rules, 1981
Published vide Notification No. F.7(6) Sansad/81, dated 22-10-1981, Published in Rajasthan Gazette, Part 4-C, Page
No. 229 (2-3) dated 22-10-1981
GSR 69. - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 read with Sub-section (11) of section 6A of the Rajasthan
Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 6 of 1957), the State
Government hereby makes the following rules to regulate the grant of advance to officers of the Rajasthan Legislative
Assembly for the purchase of Motor Car, name-

1.
Short title.
- These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Advance for purchase of Motor Car) Rules,
1981.
2.
Applicability.
- These rules shall apply to the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and also to the
Government Chief Whip and the Deputy Government Chief Whip and the Leader of the Opposition who are hereinafter
referred to as the said officers.
3.
Terms & Conditions for Advance.
- The terms and conditions on which advance may be granted for the purchase of a motor car to the said officers shall
be the same as contained in the Rajasthan Ministers (Advance for purchase of Motor Cars) Rules, 1965 for the time
being in force and the provisions of the said rules shall mutatis mutandis apply to the advance made to the said officers
for the purchase of a Motor Car.
