Bihar Development of Ayurvedic and Unani Systems of Medicine (Payment of Allowances) Rules, 1953
Published vide Notification No. 10092-L.S.G., dated 25th November, 1953
1.
These Rules may be called the Bihar Development of Ayurvedic and Unani Systems of Medicine (Payment of
Allowances) Rules, 1953.
2.

3.
The payment of travelling allowance and halting allowance will generally be regulated in accordance with the Bihar
Travelling Allowances Rules, as amended from time to time.
