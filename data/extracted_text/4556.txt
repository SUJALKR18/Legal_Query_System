The Bihar Development of Homeopathic System of Medicine (Payment of Allowances) Rules, 1955
Published vide Notification No. Ind.-M(H)-32432-H., dated Patna, the 12th November, 1955
Copy of Notification No. Ind.-M(H)-32432-H., dated Patna, the 12th November, 1955 by the Government of Bihar, Health
Department. - In exercise of powers conferred by clause (d) of sub-section (2) of Section 53 of the Bihar Development of
Homeopathic System of Medicine Act, 1953 (Bihar Act XXIV of 1953) the Governor of Bihar is pleased to make the
following Rules, namely :-
The Bihar Development of Homeopathic System of Medicine (Payment of Allowances) Rules, 1955.

The Bihar Development of Homeopathic System of Medicine (Payment of Allowances) Rules, 195...
1.
These Rules may be called the Bihar Development of Homeopathic System of Medicine (Payment of allowances) Rules,
1955.
2.
For attendance at any meeting of the Board of an Advisory Committee appointed under sub-section (i) of Section 17 of
the Bihar Development of Homeopathic System, of Medicine Act, 1953, the President and the members of the Board,
the Convenor and members of the Advisory Committee shall be paid travelling allowances at rates admissible to
Government servants of the first grade. Halting allowance shall be paid at the rate of Rs. 5 (Rupees five) only a day in
each case.
3.
The payment of travelling and halting allowances will generally be regulated in accordance with the Bihar Travelling
Allowance Rules, as amended from time to time.
