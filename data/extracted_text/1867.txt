The Auroville (Emergency Provisions) Repeal Act, 2001 Act
NO. 37 OF 2001
1167.
[4th September, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Auroville (Emergency Provisions) Act, 1980.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Auroville (Emergency Provisions) Repeal Act, 2001.
2.
Repeal of Act 59 of 1980.
