The Banking Service Commission (Repeal) Act, 2003
ACT NO. 29 OF 2003
1151.
[30th April, 2003]
An Act to repeal the Banking Service Commission Act, 1984.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-fourth Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Banking Service Commission (Repeal) Act, 2003.
2.
Repeal of Act 44 of 1984.
