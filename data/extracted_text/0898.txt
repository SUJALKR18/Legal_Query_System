Uncleared Goods(Bill of Entry) Regulations, 1972.
Published vide M.F. (D.R.) Notification No. 99, Cus, dated 26th August, 1972.
M.F. (D.R.) Notification No. 99, Cus, dated 26th August, 1972. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 157 of
the Customs Act. 1962 (52 of 1962), the Central Board of Excise and Customs hereby makes the following regulations,
namely :
1.
Short title.
- These Regulations may be called the Uncleared Goods (Bill of Entry) Regulations, 1972.
2.
Preparation of bill of entry in respect of goods to be sold at an auction.
- The person having the custody of goods that are not cleared for home consumption within the period specified in

3.
Bill of entry to be presented to the proper officer.
- This bill of entry prepared under Reg. 2 shall be deemed to be the bill of entry under sub-section (1) of Section 46 of
the said Act and presented to the proper officer.
Provided that the said bill of entry shall not be deemed to be the bill of entry in respect of goods which are not actually
sold in the auction held for the sale of goods specified in that bill of entry, and actually delivered to the buyer.
