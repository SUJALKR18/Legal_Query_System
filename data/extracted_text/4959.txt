AN ACT
To repeal the Bihar State Street Vendors (Protection of livelihood and regulation of trade ) Act, 2012.
Be it enacted by the legislature of the state of Bihar in the sixty seventh year of the Republic of India as follows :-
1.
Short title, extent and commencement.-(1) This Act may be called the Bihar State Street Vendors (Protection of
livelihood and regulation of trade ) (Repeal) Act, 2016
(2)
It shall extend to the whole of the State of Bihar.
(3)
It shall come into force at once.

2.
Repeal of Bihar State Street Vendors (Protection of livelihood and regulation of trade) Act, 2012. - The Bihar State
Street Vendors (Protection of livelihood and regulation of trade) Act 2012. (Bihar Act 25 of 2012) is hereby repealed.
3.
Saving - Not withstanding such repeal any thing done or any action taken in excercise of any power conferred by or
under the said Act shall be deemed to have been done or taken in excercise of the power conferred by or under this Act
as if this Act were inforce on the day or which such thing or action was done or taken.
