Act 4 of 2020
Published in Bihar Gazette on 20 March 2020

[This is the version of this document from 20 March 2020.]
AN ACT
TO AUTHORISE PAYMENT AND APPROPRIATION OF CERTAIN AMOUNT FROM AND OUT OF THE
CONSOLIDATED FUND OF THE STATE OF BIHAR FOR THE SERVICES DURING THE YEAR ENDING OF THE
THIRTY FIRST DAY OF MARCH, 2021.
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Bihar in the seventy first year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title, extent and commencement .-
(1)
This Act may be called the Bihar Appropriation (No.-2) Act, 2020.
(2)
It shall extend to the whole of the state of Bihar.
(3)

THE BIHAR APPROPRIATION (No.-2) ACT, 2020
It shall come into force with effect from 1st April 2020.
2.
Withdrawal of Rs. 216020,35,45,000 (Two Lakh sixteen Thousand twenty Crore thirty five Lakh forty five Thousand) out
of the Consolidated Fund of the State of Bihar for the year ending on the 31st March 2021.?From and out of the
Consolidated Fund of the Bihar, there may be paid and applied amount not exceeding those mentioned in column- 6 of
the Schedule amounting in the aggregate to the sum of Rs. 216020,35,45,000 (Two Lakh sixteen Thousand twenty
Crore thirty five Lakh forty five Thousand), only towards defraying the several charges which will come in course of
payment during the year ending on the thirty first day of March 2021, in respect of the services specified in column-2 of
the Schedule.
3.
Appropriation.? The amount authorized to be paid and applied from and out of the Consolidated Fund of the State of
Bihar by this Act shall be appropriated for and in respect of the services and purposes mentioned in the Schedule in
relation to the year ending on the thirty first day of March, 2021.
