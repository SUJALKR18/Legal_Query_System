Uttarakhand Ayurved University (Amendment) Act, 2018
(Uttarakhand Act
No. 11 of 2019
)
Last Updated 5th March, 2020
An Act further to amend the Uttarakhand Ayurved University Act, 2009 (Act 7 of 2009)
Be it enacted by the Uttarakhand State Legislative Assembly in the Seventieth Year of the Republic of India as follows -
1.
Short title, extent and commencement.
(1)
This Act may be called the Uttarakhand Ayurved University (Amendment) Act, 2018.

Uttarakhand Ayurved University (Amendment) Act, 2018
(2)
It extends to whole of the State of Uttarakhand.
(3)
It shall come into force at once.
2.
Amendment of Section 2.
- In Section 2 of the Uttarakhand Ayurved University Act, 2009 (Act 7 of 2009) (hereinafter referred to as the principal
Act) after clause (y), the following clause shall be inserted, namely-
'(z) "Registrar", "Deputy Registrar" and "Assistant Registrar" means Registrar, Deputy Registrar and Assistant Registrar
of the University respectively;"
3.
Amendment of Section 16.
- In the principal Act, title and sub-section (1) of Section 16 shall be substituted as follows, namely -
"16. Registrar, Deputy Registrar and Assistant Registrar. - (1) Registrar, Deputy Registrar and Assistant Registrar shall
be appointed by the State Government through centralised service rules for the time being in force in the State of
Uttarakhand, in such manner and on such terms and conditions as may be prescribed.".
4.
Amendment of Section 46.
- In the principal Act, Section 46 shall be renumbered as sub-section (1) thereof and after sub-section (1) so as
renumbered, the following sub-section shall be inserted, namely -
"(2) The State Government shall constitute an Admission and Fee Regulatory Committee comprising -
(a)
Retired Judge of High Court nominated by the Chief Justice of
High Court
- Chairman
(b)
Secretary, Medical Education (Ex-Officio)
- Member
(c)
Secretary, Law (Ex-Officio)
- Member
(d)
A Retired Government Officer retired from a post not below the
rank of Secretary to the State Government nominated by the State
Government
- Member
(e)
One Ex Vice-Chancellor of the State Ayush University nominated
by the Governor
- Member
(f)
Two Eminent Ayush Educationist nominated by the State
Government
- Member
(g)
Secretary, Ayush and Ayush Education
-Secretary
A renowned Chartered Accountant as defined in the rules shall also be nominated by the Chairman of the Committee.
Notwithstanding anything in other Act/Ordinance, this section shall prevail.".
