The Code of Civil Procedure (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 1970
Tamil Nadu Act
15 of 1970
Statement of Objects and Reasons. - Under sub-section (1) of section 123 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, a Rule
Committee shall be constituted at the town which is the usual place of sitting of each of the High Courts referred to in
section 122. Clause (d) of sub-section (2) of section 123 of the said Code, inter alia, provides that such Committee shall,
in the towns of Calcutta, Madras and Bombay consist of an attorney. The High Court, Madras considers that the number
of attorneys has dwindled considerably in the Madras High Court and it is very difficult to find a suitable person to fill up
the vacancy in the Rule Committee from among the attorneys. The High Court, Madras has, therefore, suggested that
the Code of Civil Procedure may, suitably, be amended so as to provide for the appointment of an advocate member
instead of an attorney member.

The Code of Civil Procedure (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 1970
2. The Bill seeks to achieve the above object.
Published in Part IV-Section 3 of the Tamil Nadu Government Gazette Extraordinary, dated the 13th September 1970.
Received the assent of the President on the 14th April 1970 and published in Part IV-Section 4 of the Tamil Nadu
Government Gazette Extraordinary, dated the 10th June 1970.
An Act to further amend the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, in its application to the State of Tamil Nadu.
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Tamil Nadu in the Twentieth Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title and extent.
- This Act may be called the Code of Civil Procedure (Tamil Nadu Amendment) Act, 1970.
(2)
It extends to the whole of the State of Tamil Nadu.
2.
Amendment of section 123, Central Act V of 1908.
- In sub-section (2) of section 123 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Central Act V of 1908),-
(a)
in clause (b), for the words "two legal practitioners", the words "three legal practitioners" shall be substituted;
(b)
in clause (d), the word "Madras" shall be omitted.
