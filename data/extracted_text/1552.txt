The Cotton Transport (Repeal) Act, 1995
(Act
No. 19 of 1995
)
1851.
11th.
May, 1995
An Act to repeal the Cotton Transport Act, 1923.
Be it enacted by Parliament in the Forty-Sixth Year of the Republic of India as follows :-
1.
Short title. - This Act may be called the Cotton Transport (Repeal) Act, 1995.

Repeal of Act 3 of 1923. - The Cotton Transport Act, 1923 is hereby repealed.
