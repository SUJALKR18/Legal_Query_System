The Indian Administrative Service (Fixation of Cadre Strength) Regulations, 1955
Last Updated 11th October, 2019
In pursuance of Sub-rule (1) of rule 4 of the Indian Administrative Service (Cadre) Rules, 1954, the Central Government
in consultation with the Governments of the State concerned hereby makes the following regulations, namely:-
1.
These regulations may be called the Indian Administrative Service (Fixation of Cadre Strength) Regulations, 1955.
2.
The posts borne on, and the strength and composition of the cadre of the Indian Administrative Service of the various
States shall be as specified in the Schedule to these regulations.
