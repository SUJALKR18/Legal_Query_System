Limits of the Port of Tuticorin
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 3(E), 3rd January, 2006
Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport and Highways
(Department of Shipping)
(Ports Wing)
G.S.R. 3(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 5 of the Indian Parts Act, 1908, (15 of 1908) read with
clause (g) of Section 2 of Major Port Trusts Act, 1963 (38 of 1963) and in supersession of the notification of the
Government of India in the erstwhile Ministry of Surface Transport, (Ports Wing) number G.S.R. 60(E), dated the 6th
February, 1997, published in the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 3, Sub section (i) dated the 6th February, 1997, the
Central Government hereby declares that the limits of the Port of Tuticorin as set out in the Schedule, for the purpose of
first mentioned Act shall be as under :-
