1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Coinage of Two Hundred and Fifty Rupees to commemorate the occasion of 250th
Session of Rajya Sabha Rules, 2019.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Denomination of Coin.
- The coin of Two Hundred and Fifty Rupees denomination shall be coined at the Mint for issue under the authority of
the Central Government on the occasion of "250th Session of Rajya Sabha".
3.
Dimension and composition of coin.
- The coin of the above denomination mentioned in rule 2 shall conform to the following dimensions and composition,
