5.
Record of criminal conviction sufficient for compelling refund of property corruptly taken or extorted.
- From and after the date of this Regulation, it shall not be necessary for any party from whom money or property may
have been corruptly taken or extorted to institute a civil action for the recovery thereof but, on proof of the charge in a
criminal prosecution for these offences, a certified copy of the conviction by [the Court]
