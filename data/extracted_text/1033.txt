The Bill of Coastal Goods (Form) Regulations, 1976
Published vide Cus. Notification, No. 425 (G.S.R. 1553), dated 30th October, 1976
Cus. Notification, No. 425 (G.S.R. 1553), dated 30th October, 1976. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section
157, read with Section 92 of the Customs Act, 1962 (
52 of 1962
), the Central Board of Excise and Customs hereby makes the following regulation, namely :
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These regulations may be called the Bill of Coastal Goods (Form) Regulations, 1976.
(2)
