The Chennai City Municipal Corporation Licensing (Display of Sign Board) Rules, 1976
City Municipal Corporation Licensing (Display of Sign Board) Rules, the same having been previously published as
required by clause (a) of section 348 of the said Act.
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the [Chennai]
[Substituted for the word 'Madras' by the City of Madras (Alteration of Name) Act, 19% (Tamil Nadu Act 28 of 1996).]
City Municipal Corporation Licensing (Display of Sign Board) Rules, 1976.
(2)
These rules shall come into force at once.
2.
Power to impose condition as to display.
- The Commissioner, Corporation of [Chennai]
[Substituted for the word 'Madras' by the City of Madras (Alteration of Name) Act, 19% (Tamil Nadu Act 28 of 1996).]
shall require any person seeking a licence or permission to use any place, within the limits of the city for any of the
purposes enumerated in Schedule VI to the [Chennai]
[Substituted for the word 'Madras' by the City of Madras (Alteration of Name) Act, 19% (Tamil Nadu Act 28 of 1996).]
City Municipal Corporation Act, 1919 (Tamil Nadu Act IV of 1919) and also to construct or establish any factory,
workshop, or work place in which it is proposed to employ steam power, water power, or other mechanical power or
electric power, to display the sign board of the shop, the factory, workshop or workplace in Tamil:
Provided that the use of any other languages, if so desired by the person concerned, shall not be objected to, if the
display, which shall not be larger than Tamil, in such other languages finds/find place immediately below the display in
Tamil.
