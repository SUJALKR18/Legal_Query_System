The Companies (Fees on Applications) Rules, 1999
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 501(E), dated 6th July, 1999
Ministry of Law, Justice and Company Affairs
(Department of Company Affairs)
G.S.R. 501(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 642, read with sub-section (2) of
section 637A, of the Companies Act, 1956 (1 of 1956), and in supersession of the Companies (Fees on Applications)
Rules, 1968, the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:
1.
Short title.
(1)
These Rules may be called the Companies (Fees on Applications) Rules, 1999.
