The Madras Port Trust (General Provident Fund) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 47(E), 3rd February, 2006
Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport and Highways
(Department of Shipping)
(Ports Wing)
G.S.R. 47(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 124, read with Sub-section (1) of
Section 132 of the Major Port Trusts Act, 1963 (38 of 1963), the Central Government hereby approves the Madras Port
Trust (General Provident Fund) Amendment Regulations, 2006 made by the Board of Trustees of Madras Port Trust as
set out in the Schedule annexed to this Notification.

The Madras Port Trust (General Provident Fund) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006
2.
The said Regulations shall come into force from the date of publication of this Notification in the Official Gazette.
Schedule
Chennai Port Trust
In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 28 of the Major Port Trusts Act, 1963 (38 of 1963), the Madras Port
Trust Board hereby makes the following Amendment Regulations :-
1.
Short Title.
- These Regulations shall be called the Madras Port Trust (General Provident Fund) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006.
2.
These Regulations shall come into force from the date of publication of the Regulation in the Official Gazette.
3.
In the Madras Port Trust (General Provident Fund) Regulations, 1997, the existing Regulation "5-Provident Fund
Reserve Fund" shall be deleted.
Foot Note :

subsequently amended vide Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport and Highways's Notification No. G.S.R. 51(E) dated
17-1-2000.
