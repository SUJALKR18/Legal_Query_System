Tamil Nadu Lapsed Acts (Removal of Doubts) Act, 1948
(Tamil Nadu Act
5 of 1948
)
Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 1969, as amended by the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws (Second Amendment)
Order, 1969
Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 1970
Received the assent of the Governor on the 26th April 1948 and first published in the Fort St. George Gazette
Extraordinary, dated the 27th April 1948.
An Act for the removal of doubts regarding the operation of certain enactments.
whereas, the Acts specified in the Schedule below, being Acts made by the Governor of Madras under a Proclamation

----- PAGE 2 -----
Tamil Nadu Lapsed Acts (Removal of Doubts) Act, 1948
issued by him under section 93 of the Government of India Act, 1935 (26 Geo. 5, Ch. 2), will cease to have effect on the
30th day of April 1948 on the lapse of two years from the date on which the said Proclamation was revoked;
And Whereas, it is expedient to remove some doubts regarding the operation of those Acts;
It is hereby enacted as follows:-
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
This Act may be called the [Tamil Nadu]
[Substituted for the word 'Madras' by the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 1969, as amended by the Tamil Nadu
Adaptation of Laws (Second Amendment) Order, 1969.]
Lapsed Acts (Removal of Doubts) Act, 1948.
(2)
It shall come into force on the 29th day of April 1948.
2.
Consequences of expiry of Acts specified in the Schedule.
- Upon the expiry of the Acts specified in the Schedule-
(a)
section 8 of the [Tamil Nadu]
[Substituted for the word 'Madras' by the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 1969, as amended by the Tamil Nadu
Adaptation of Laws (Second Amendment) Order, 1969.]
General Clauses Act, 1891 ([Tamil Nadu]
[Substituted for the expression 'Madras Act by the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 197U, which was deemed to
have come into force on the 14th January 1969.]
Act I of 1981), shall apply, as if the Acts so specified had then been repealed by a [Tamil Nadu Act]
[Substituted for the expression 'Madras Act' by the Tamil Nadu Adaptation of Laws Order, 1970, which was deemed to
have come into force on the 14th January 1969.]
;
(b)
save as provided in clause (a), the Acts, if any, amended by the Acts so specified shall have operation as if the latter
Acts had never been enacted.
