The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1981
Published vide Notification No. F.7(5) Sansad/ 81, dated 22-10-1981, Published in Rajasthan Gazette, Extraordinary
Part 4-C, Page 229(1) dated 22-10-1981
G.S.R. 68. - In exercise of the powers conferred by section 11 read with Sub-section (7) and (8) of section 6A of the
Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 6 of 1957)
the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:-
1.
Short title.
- These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Officers (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1981.

2.
Applicability.
- These rules shall apply to the Speaker and Deputy Speaker of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and also to the
Government Chief Whip and the Deputy Government Chief Whip and the Leader of the Opposition who are hereinafter
referred to as the said officers.
3.
Medical Facilities.
- The officers shall be entitled to the same medical facilities and attendance as are admissible to a Minister under the
Rajasthan Ministers (Medical Attendance) Rules, 1961 for the time being in force and the provisions of the said rules
shall [mutatis mutandis] apply to the said officers in relation to the medical facilities and attendance admissible to them.
