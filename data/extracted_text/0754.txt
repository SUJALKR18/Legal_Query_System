Published vide Notification Gazette of India , 1965, part 2 section 3(ii), page 3893.

Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 (44 of 1958), the Central Government hereby directs that, with effect from the 1st January,
1966, there shall be levied on the passage money paid by every overseas passenger proceeding on a voyage
