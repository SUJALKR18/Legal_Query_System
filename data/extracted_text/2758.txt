The Customs Baggage Declaration Regulations, 2013
Published vide Notification New Delhi, the 29th August, 2013, No. 90/2013-Customs (N.T.)
Ministry of Finance
(Department of Revenue)
G.S.R. 584(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (a) of Section 81 of the Customs Act, 1962, the Central
Board of Excise and Customs hereby makes the following regulations, namely :-
1.
Short title.
- These regulations may be called the Customs Baggage Declaration Regulations, 2013.
2.
Extent of application.

The Customs Baggage Declaration Regulations, 2013
- These regulations shall apply to baggage including any package comprised therein of the passengers coming to India.
These regulations will come into effect w.e.f. 1st January, 2014.
