Bihar Act
17 of 1939
Published in the Bihar Gazette of the 22nd November, 1939.
An Act to amend the Court-fees Act, 1870, in its application to Bihar.
Whereas it is expedient to amend the Court-fees Act, 1870, in its application to the Province of Bihar in the manner
hereinafter appearing;
It is hereby enacted as follows:-
