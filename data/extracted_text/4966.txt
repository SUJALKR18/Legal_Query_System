Bihar Police Driver Cadre Rules, 2017
Published vide Notification No-8/mis.-10-74/2017-7655/ H.P, dated 20.9.2017
No-8/mis.-10-74/2017-7655/ H.P. - In exercise of powers conferred under the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution of
India read with section 55 and section 97 of Bihar Police Act, 2007, the Governor of Bihar is hereby pleased to make the
following Rules to regulate the recruitment and service conditions of Bihar Police Driver Cadre :-
1.
Short title, extent and commencement.
(1)
The Rules shall be called Bihar Police Driver Cadre Rules, 2017.
(2)
It shall extend to whole state of Bihar.

(3)
It shall come into force with immediate effect.
2.
Definition.
- In the Rules, unless the context otherwise requires-
(a)
"Board" means The Central Selection Board (Constable Recruitment);
(b)
"Cadre Controlling Authority" means The Director General of Police, Bihar;
(c)
"Appointing Authority" means Senior Superintendent of Police/ Superintendent of Police/Commandant/Officers of
equivalent rank.
(d)
"Cadre" means Bihar Police Driver Cadre.
3.
Constitution of Cadre.
- The following posts shall be in this Cadre :-
(i)
Inspector of Police (Transport);
(ii)
Sub-Inspector of Police (Transport);
(iii)
Assistant Sub Inspector of Police (Transport);
(iv)
Havildar Driver;
(v)
Driver Constable.
4.
Strength of Cadre.
- The strength of this cadre shall be according to the present strength, but, the cadre-strength shall be deem to be
revised due to creation or abolition of posts by the State Government as per requirement from time to time accordingly.
All the personnel of this cadre will wear uniform, but they will not be absorbed in general police cadres. All the personnel
of Reserve Sub-Inspector (Transport), Havildar Driver and Driver Constable already working before come in to force of
these Rules shall automatically be deemed to be the member of this cadre. The basic post of appointment in this cadre
shall only be the post of Driver Constable. Apart from Driver Constables and Havildar Drivers, Assistant Sub Inspector of
Police (Transport) will also perform the work of motor driving.
5.
Recruitment eligibility in the cadre.
(1)
In July, the Director General of Police, Bihar will get calculated the number of reservation category-wise vacant posts of
Driver Constables to be filled up by direct recruitment.
(2)
The selection of candidates for the appointment to the posts of Driver Constables to be filled up by direct recruitment
shall be made by the Central Selection Board (Constable Recruitment) through separate/ Combined Competitive
Examination. For the appointment to post of Driver Constables Combined Examination may be held jointly with the
examination to be held for the appointment of Constables. A requisition shall be sent to the Central Selection Board
(Constable Recruitment) in the prescribed proforma, in which information regarding number of vacant posts and
category-wise reservation will also be mentioned. The Central Selection Board (Constable Recruitment) shall set
question papers, conduct examinations, appoint examiners to examine the answer-sheets and conduct physical
eligibility test and eligibility test of motor vehicle driving and shall send the recommended list of successful candidates.
(3)

The Board shall advertise the number of vacancies in widely circulated news papers and through website as per
requisition received from the Director General of Police, Bihar. The candidates shall abide by the directions and
conditions given in the advertisements. Examination fees shall be charged from the candidates, which may be fixed by
the Board. The candidates shall submit their application forms in the prescribed proforma online through the website as
per the terms and conditions published by the Board in the advertisement. In case of Combined Examination candidates
fulfilling the determined qualifications will have to give their preference for the selection of posts.
(4)
The Board shall hold Written Competitive Examination for all the candidates in the first stage. The paper of written
examination shall be based on multiple choice questions. There will be one paper of 100 questions of 100 marks and the
examination period shall be of two hours. One mark will be given for each correct answer. Candidates securing less than
30 marks in this paper shall be declared unsuccessful to appear for physical eligibility test. In the said paper, questions
related to the General Knowledge and Current issues will asked. 05 times successful candidates to existing vacancies
shall be selected reservation category-wise, on the basis of marks obtained in the written examination. Answer-sheets
shall be in two copies, in which one copy shall be kept as reserve in the Board for one year after declaration of result but
if any case is under consideration in the court related to answer sheet of the examination will be kept as reserve till the
disposal of the case. In case of unavailability of sufficient number of eligible candidates for the physical eligibility test, the
Board may reduce the above mentioned proportion suitably.
(5)
Required Physical Eligibility Tests of the selected candidates on the basis of written exam held in First Phase will taken
itself by the Board in which it will be compulsory to qualify only. Determined measurements and other required tests in
Physical Eligibility Tests, transparent procedure based on electronic and computerized methods based on modern
technology will be adopted. Videography of the entire procedure will also be made. The Board shall inform all the
candidates regarding their success and failure provisionally in PET everyday and the unsuccessful candidates shall
require to put their signatures on the Master-Chart. Any candidate, dissatisfied with the result of PET, shall place his
objections before the Board within 03 days. The Board shall, after hearing the representations related to the objections,
dispose of the same on the date of receipt of the objections.
(6)
The successful candidates in the physical eligibility test shall be given an opportunity to appear in the eligibility test of
motor driving as, per the provisions prescribed in this Rules.
(7)
Appointment to the post of Driver Constable will be made by Senior Superintendent of Police/Superintendent of
Police/Commandant/Officers of equivalent rank on the basis of list of recommendation received from the Board.

7.
Preparation of merit list.
(1)
Reservation category-wise merit list of successful candidates in all tests will be prepared according to vacancies on the
basis of marks obtained in efficiency test of motor driving.
(2)
In case of holding the Combined Competitive Examination separate merit list of successful candidates for the posts will
be prepared on the basis of preference indicated by the candidates.
(3)
If two or more than two candidates secure equal marks in the efficiency test relating to motor driving, their places in the
merit list shall be determined on the basis of their date of birth. This is to say that candidate senior in age shall remain
above in order of merit. In case of similar date of birth of two or more than two candidates, their Place shall be
determined on the basis of their educational qualifications. It means the candidate having higher educational
qualifications, shall remain above in order of merit. After that also, if more than one candidate are on same footage their
inter-se ranks shall be determined on the basis of marks obtained in the qualifying examination. It means candidates
having more marks will remain above in merit order. After that if the seniority of candidates is not determined even after
above provisions it will be determined on the basis of English alphabet of their names as mentioned in their certificate of
Tenth Board.
(4)
The minimum qualifying marks for the candidates of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes shall be 33.5%, for the
candidates of Backward class and Extremely Backward class 35% and for the candidates of General category 40% for
the final merit list.
(5)
Board shall prepare a list of candidates on the basis of the merit in descending order and shall recommend the names
the candidates reservation category-wise in descending order of the marks according to the requisition received from the
Director General of Police.
(6)
Board shall publish Supplementary lists will be published up to 06 months on the request of Director General of Police,
Bihar to appoint to the vacant posts under the advertisement occurring due to non-joining of candidates. 06 months
period will be counted from the date of publication of merit list.
8.
Medical Test, character and antecedent verification.
(1)
Before the appointment to the post of Driver Constable, the Appointing Authority will get Medical Test as per form
no-103 of the successful candidates, and their character and antecedent's verification as per the form no-101 of the
Bihar Police Manual 1978 will made by the Appointing Authority. On adverse report or/and in case of concealment of
facts by the candidates, concerned candidates shall be declared unfit for appointment.

(2)
According to the provisions made in the Bihar Police Manual Rule 658 (iv), candidates must be free from disease,
constitutional affliction or physical infirmity due to which he is or may be unfit for duty.
Note-1:-All the selected candidates will get to be medically test before the appointment. Eye sight, colour blindness,
hearing power, stammering, flat-foot and knocking knee etc. will also be test in the medical test. It will be compulsory to
be found the selected candidates medically fit for the appointment.
Note-2:-Due to defect like Stuttering and stammering the candidate will not be eligible for selection.
9.
Educational qualification.
(1)
The minimum educational qualification for the recruitment to the basic post of Driver Constable shall be intermediate
(10+2) Pass or equivalent.
(2)
It will be compulsory for the candidate to holder of license of Light Motor Vehicle (LMV)/ Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV)
issued by a competent authority minimum one year before the date of issue of the advertisement.
10.
Age.
- The age-limit of the candidates will be as follows :-
(a)
For candidates of General Category minimum 18 years and maximum 25 years;
(b)
For male candidates of Backward Class Category and Extremely Backward Class Category, minimum 18 years and
maximum 27 years;
(c)
For female candidates of Backward Class Category and Extremely Backward Class Category, minimum 18 years and
maximum 28 years;
(d)
For male or female candidates of Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes categories, minimum 18 years and maximum 30
years
11.
Reservation.
- Reservation provision as directed by the State Government, from time to time will be applicable to the appointment and
promotion to the posts of Driver Constable.
12.
Training.
(1)
The basic training of Driver Constable shall be of 180 working days. The syllabus of the training shall be determined by
D.G. (Training), with the approval of D.G.P., Bihar which will include technical and mechanical training.
(2)
For the promotion from Assistant Sub-Inspector (Transport) to Sub- Inspector (Transport), training shall be of 100
working days. The syllabus for this training shall be determined by D.G. (Training), with the approval of D.G.P., Bihar.
13.
Probation period and Confirmation.
(1)
The newly recruited Driver Constables shall be on probation with effect from the date of appointment. The period of
probation shall be of three years, which may be extended for one year on being found service and performance
unsatisfactory. This kind of extension may be made maximum once in the service. In case of unsatisfactory service even
after the extended period Driver Constable appointed on probation will be terminated from the service.
(2)
On the basis of successful completion of the probation period and passing of the Departmental Examination, the Driver
Constable may be confirmed in the service.

14.
Seniority.
- The State inter-se seniority of the Driver Constables shall be determined according to the total marks obtained in
eligibility test of motor driving efficiency test conducted by the Board and marks obtained in exams of basic training but
before coming into force of these Rules, those personnels who are already in service, their inter-se seniority shall remain
.....affected and they shall be deemed to be included automatically in this cadre.
15.
Promotion.
(1)
The promotional chain of the confirmed employees in this cadre shall be as per the following :-
(a)
Driver Constable to Havildar Driver;
(b)
Havildar Driver to Assistant Sub-Inspector(Transport);
(c)
From Assistant Sub-Inspector(Transport) to the post of Sub-Inspector (Transport);
(d)
From Sub-Inspector(Transport) to the post of Inspector(Transport);
(2)
The promotion shall be admissible on the basis of seniority cum eligibility criterion which shall be considered as per
recommendation of the Departmental Promotion Committee. Secret Character Remark, Performance Report, required
Trainings, Departmental Examinations will be included in the suitable eligibility criterion.
16.
Kalawadhi.
- The kalawadhi for promotion in Driver cadre will be as follows:-
(a)
Only those Driver Constables will be promoted to the rank of Havildar Driver whose service period of six years is
completed.
(b)
Only those Havildar Driver will be promoted to the rank of Assistant Sub- Inspector of Police (Transport) whose service
period of 15 years is completed.
(c)
Only those Assistant Sub-Inspector of Police (Transport) will be promoted to the rank of Sub-Inspector of Police
(Transport) who have acquired training of driving of vehicle, maintenance, technical skill and latest rules of traffic etc.
and whose service period of 22 years is completed.
(d)
Only those Sub-Inspector of Police (Transport) will be promoted to the rank of Inspector of Police (Transport) whose
Service Period of 30 years is completed.
(e)
The promotion may be considered on completion of scale-based Kalawadhi determined by the State Government/
General Administrative Department, from time to time.
17.
Residual Matters.
- Any matter, not covered by the Rules or Order/Orders issued there under shall be governed by the provisions of the
Bihar Service Code/Rules /Resolutions/Manual(s) which applicable to the equivalent Posts in the State Government.
18.
Explanation.
- If any doubt arises regarding any provision of the Rules, the interpretation made by the Home Department (Police
Branch) related there to after consultation with the Law Department, shall be final.
19.
Repeal and Savings.

(1)
The provisions laid down in relevant Rules and Appendix-88 of Bihar Police Manual, 1978 regarding examination for
recruitment and appointment/ posting of the Driver Constables, the orders/circulars /notification/instructions issued
earlier in this regard are hereby repealed.
(2)
Not with standing such repeal, above Rules being framed, the decisions taken earlier and the resolution/Rules/orders
issued earlier and the actions taken in the light of the same, shall be deemed to be done under the Rules or done under
the power conferred under the Rules, as if the Rules were in force on such date on which such thing was done or such
action was taken.
