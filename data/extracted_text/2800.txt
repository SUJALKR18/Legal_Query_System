The Coinage of One Hundred Rupees and Five Rupees Coins to mark the Occasion of Centenary ...
G.S.R. 697(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of Section 24 of the
Coinage Act, 2011 (
11 of 2011
), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely :-
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Coinage of One Hundred Rupees and Five Rupees Coins to mark the occasion of
Centenary Commemoration of Komagata Maru Incident Rules, 2014.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Denomination of coins.
- The coins of the following denominations shall also be coined at the Mint for issue under the authority of the Central
Government to commemorate the occasion of "Centenary Commemoration of Komagata Maru Incident", namely:-
(i)
One Hundred Rupees;
(ii)
Five Rupees.
