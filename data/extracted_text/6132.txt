The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Members (Telephone Facility) Rules, 1982
Published vide Notification No. F.7(3) Sansad/81, dated 1-3-1982, published in Rajpatra (Extraordinary), part 4(ga),
dated 1-3-1982, page 444
Last Updated 24th May, 2019
In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (e) of sub-section (2) of section 11 read with sub-section (1) of section 9
of the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 6 of
1957), the State Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:-
1.
Short title.
Page 1/2
----- PAGE 2 -----
The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Members (Telephone Facility) Rules, 1982
- These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Members (Telephone Facility) Rules, 1982.
2.
Definition.
- In these rules unless there is anything repugnant to the subject or context, "member" means a member of the
Rajasthan Legislative Assembly, but does not include-
(i)
a Minister as defined in the Rajasthan Minister's Salaries Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 43 of 1956):
(ii)
and the officer of the Legislative Assembly for the State as defined in the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly (Officers and
Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 6 of 1957); and
(iii)
A Government Chief Whip or a Deputy Government Chief Whip or a Leader of the Opposition as defined in the
enactment referred to in sub-clause (ii).
3.
Telephone Allowance.
- A member shall be entitled to draw a telephone allowance of [Rs. 5000/- ]
[Substituted 'Rs. 4000/-' by Notification No. G.S.R. 16, dated 12.5.2005, (w.e.f. 30.3.1998).]
p.m. if he maintains a telephone at his residence in his own name.
