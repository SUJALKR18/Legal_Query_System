The Merchant Shipping (Recruitment And Placement Of Seafarers) Rules, 2005
Short title and commencement .-(1) These rules may be called The Merchant Shipping (Recruitment and Placement of
Seafarers) Rules, 2005.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Definitions .-In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires,--
(a)
"Act" means the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 (
44 of 1958
);
(b)
"advisory board" means the advisory board or other authority constituted by the Central Government under clause (a) of
sub--section (3) of section 95 of the Act;
(c)
"Director" means the Director of the seamen's employment office concerned or any other officer authorised by the
Director-General in this behalf;
(d)
"Director-General" means the "Director-General of Shipping" appointed under section 7 of the Act;
(e)
"employer" means any person, who has assumed the responsibility of employing seafarers for himself or for any other
person in respect of a ship and who has agreed to take over all such duties and responsibilities;
(f)
"Form" means a Form appended to these rules;
(g)
"inspecting authority" means any officer subordinate to the Director--General or any other person or body of persons
professionally competent to carry out inspection under rule 3 and authorised by the Director-General in this behalf;
(h)
"license" means the recruitment and placement service license issued under rule 4;
(i)
"ship owner" means the owner of a ship or any organisation or any person such as manager, agent or bareboat
charterer, who or which has assumed the responsibility for operation of the ship from such owner and who or which on
assuming such responsibilities had agreed to take over all the duties and responsibilities as such owner.
(j)
[ society, means the Seafarers Welfare Fund Society as defined in the Merchant Shipping (Seamen's levy of fees rules)
1974]
3.
Inspection .-(1) The inspecting authority shall carry out the inspection of recruitment and placement service so as to
ensure that--
(a)
no fees or other charges for recruitment or for providing employment to seafarers are borne directly or indirectly, in
whole or in part, by the seafarer:
Provided that the cost of the requisite medical examination or requisite certificates or passport or visa or continuous
discharge certificate shall be borne by the seafarer;
(b)
the seafarers' personal data is collected, stored, processed, combined and communicated to third parties:
Provided that the right to privacy of the seafarer and the need to protect confidentiality as per general employment
practices shall be maintained;
(c)
the management and staff are adequately trained persons having relevant knowledge of the maritime industry to the
extent of the duties assigned;
(d)

no means, mechanism or list is used with the intention to prevent or deter seafarers from gaining employment;
(e)
the employment contract or agreement contains a provision to repatriate the seafarer in the event of his being stranded
at any place or transportation of the mortal remains of an expired seafarer:
Provided that the employment contract or agreement shall be in accordance with any law for the time being in force and
collective agreements, if any:
Provided further that the seafarer is informed of his rights and duties under the employment contract or agreement prior
to placement:
Provided also that the seafarer shall be supplied with a copy of employment contract or agreement and the terms of
such contract or agreement shall be explained to him before he signs the same;
(f)
the provisions relating to prohibition against discrimination contained in section 97-A of the Act are not violated;
(g)
records of all seafarers recruited are maintained;
(h)
a seafarer recruited or placed is qualified and holds the requisite documents for the job concerned;
(i)
a proper grievance redressal mechanism is adopted in regard to any complaint made against the recruitment and
placement service or against the ship owner:
Provided that any unresolved grievance against the recruitment and placement service or the ship owner shall be
brought to the notice of the Director-General or the country to which the ship belongs, as the case may be:
Provided further that any complaint against a recruitment and placement service or a ship owner may be directly
referred by the seafarer to the Director-General or the country to which the ship belongs;
(j)
the seamen's employment office concerned and next of kin of the seafarer is informed of each death or disability of the
seafarer within forty-eight hours of such death or disability in Form V;
(k)
the details of the employer and the ship owner are maintained in respect of each ship;
(l)
an acknowledgement containing the name, address, license number and its validity is issued to the seafarer on filing an
application for placement;
(m)
the responsibility, authority and inter-relation of all the personnel in the recruitment and placement service who manage,
perform and verify the work are defined and adequate resources and infrastructure are available to carry out their
functions.
(2)
On completion of an inspection under sub-rule (1), the inspecting authority shall submit a report to the Director within a
period of fifteen days:
Provided that the Director-General may permit such inspection authority to submit a report within a further period not
exceeding fifteen days.
(3)
Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-rule (2) or sub-rule (4), the Director-General may require any inspecting
authority to submit a report to him directly or cause any other inspections to be done as he deems fit.
(4)
The Director shall ensure that annual inspections or inspections otherwise required by these rules are carried out by the
inspecting authority.
(5)
The recruitment and placement service shall pay a fee of ten thousand rupees for every inspection under these rules.
(6)
Where the inspecting authority is an officer subordinate to the Director--General, the recruitment and placement service
shall remit the fee referred to in sub-rule (5) by way of crossed demand draft drawn on a scheduled bank and payable at

5.
Functions of seamen's employment office .-(1) The seamen's employment office shall-
(a)
issue or renew a license referred to in rule 4 on receipt of authorisation from the Director-General;
(b)
maintain a register of license and to assign distinct license numbers and to display the details of license on the website;
(c)
maintain a register of recruitment and placement service to whom license is rejected, or suspended or cancelled and the
details thereof and display it on website;
(d)
maintain the details of complaints received from seafarers concerning working or living conditions on board a ship;
(e)
refer, where required, the complaints concerning the activities of the recruitment and placement service from seafarers
to the advisory board;
(f)
maintain details of information received in cases of death or disability of seafarers;
(g)
maintain registers of seafarers in respect of various categories of seafarers;
(h)
maintain a register for [quarterly reports] received from the recruitment and placement service in Form IV;
(2)
Where the recruitment and placement service fails to repatriate the seafarer in the event of his being stranded or fails to
transport the mortal remains of an expired seafarer, the bank guarantee may be utilised by the seamen's employment
office for the aforesaid purposes and also for the maintenance of the stranded seafarer.

6.
Suspension or withdrawal of recruitment and placement service license .-(1) On a report by the inspecting authority or a
complaint by a seafarer or otherwise, the Director-General may authorise the Director to issue a notice in Form VII to the
recruitment and placement service requiring it to show cause within a period of thirty days from the date of issue of such
notice as to why the license shall not be suspended or withdrawn.
(2)
The Director-General, after considering the reply to the show-cause notice, shall as soon as possible but not later than
thirty days from the date of receipt of reply, if any, authorise the Director to pass such order of suspension or withdrawal
as he deems fit and communicate the same to the recruitment and placement service and forward a copy thereof
alongwith a detailed report to the Central Government forthwith.
(3)
Every order passed under sub-rule (2) shall be self-contained and give reasons for the conclusions stated therein for
suspension or withdrawal of license.
7.
Appeal .-Any person aggrieved of an order under rule 6 may within thirty days of the date of receipt of such order appeal
to the Central Government, which shall pass an order thereon within thirty days from the date of receipt of such appeal.
8.
Transitional provision .-Notwithstanding anything contained in the foregoing rules, a person, company, institution,
agency or other organisation in the public or private sector which is engaged in recruiting seafarers on behalf of
employers or placing seafarers with employers may obtain a license [on or before 31st December, 2006] from the date
of commencement of these rules:
Provided that a person, company, institution, agency or other organisation in the public or private sector may continue to
function as such till such person, company, institution, agency or other organization obtains a license under these rules.
