Rules for Protection of Forests From Fire, 1969
Published vide Notification No. For-5 (Misc 43/67/(5), dated the 1st March, 1969
Last Updated 18th February, 2020
Notification No. For-5 (Misc 43/67/(5), dated the 1st March, 1969. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 24 (b)
and (c) of the Nagaland Forest Act, 1968 (Act No. 3 1968), the Governor of Nagaland is pleased to make the following
rules to protect the Government Reserved Forests from fire within the State of Nagaland.
These rules shall come into force with immediate effect. These rules shall apply to all areas to which the Nagaland
Forest Act, 1968, applies.
1.
No person shall, within a forest reserve which is specially protected from fire or along its boundaries, kindle or carry any
fire, or leave any fire burning otherwise than as provided in Rule 11.

2.
Between the 1st February and 31st May no person desirous of clearing by fire any standing forest or grass land within
half a mile of the boundary of the forest reserves which is specially protected from fire, or of burning rubbish within such
distance of such reserve, shall do so otherwise than in accordance with the following rules:
(i)
Notice of such desire shall be at least one week beforehand to the nearest local Range Forest Official, or to whoever
may be in-charge of the range office at the time ;
(ii)
He shall choose for such burning a day or time when a high wind is not blowing;
(iii)
He shall take every precaution that the fire does not spread beyond the limits of the land to be burnt over, or of the
heaps into which the rubbish to be burnt may have been made, and in case of accident shall render every assistance in
preventing the fire from spreading into the reserve.
3.
Any person collecting inflammable forest produce such as grass or bamboos, on land adjoining a forest reserve and any
holder of a permit to collect such produce from the forest reserve, shall stock the material so collected in an open space.
4.
All persons travelling on roads, paths or tracks passing through or along the boundary of a forest reserve which is
specially protected from fire, shall camp only at such places as may be cleared and set apart for such purpose by the
Divisional Forest Officer, who shall yearly publish a list of such camping grounds. Camping at localities other than those
so set apart along such roads, paths or tracks is prohibited. Persons using such camping grounds shall light fires they
may require for cooking or other purposes in such way as not to endanger the forest reserve or the buildings or other
property, on the camping grounds.
Eviction from Reserved Forests under Section 72 (c)
1. Subject as hereinafter provided, the Divisional Forest Officer may eject any person from land in a reserved forest.
2. Such person may be ejected or ordered to vacate forthwith, and the Divisional Forest Officer may sell, confiscate or
destroy any crop raised, or any building or other construction erected without authority
3. Any person of persons intentionally disobeying an order to vacate under sub-rule(2) shall, without prejudice to
appropriate action under Sections 23 and 24 of the Act be liable to a penalty which may extend to two hundred rupees,
and, in case such disobedience is continued, to a further penalty which may extend to fifty rupees for each day during
which such breach continues.
