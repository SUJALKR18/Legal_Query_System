Andhra Pradesh Transmission/Distribution/ Trading of Electricity Licence Application Fee Rules, 2004
Published vide Notification No. G.O. Ms. No. 48, Energy (P.R. III), dated 22.3.2004
Last Updated 21st August, 2019
No. G.O. Ms. No. 48. - In exercise of the Powers conferred by Section 180 read with Section 15 of the Electricity Act
2003 Act No. 36 of 2003 the Governor of Andhra Pradesh hereby makes the following rules.
1.
Short title and commencement.

(a)
These rules may be called the Andhra Pradesh Transmission/Distribution/ Trading of Electricity Licence Application Fee
Rules, 2004.
(b)
These rules shall come into force on and from the date of publication in the Andhra Pradesh Gazette.
2.
Definitions.
(a)
"Act" means the Electricity Act 2003
(b)
"Commission" means the Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (APERC)
(c)
Words and expressions used but not defined in these Rules have the same meaning assigned to them in the Act.
3.
Licence application fee.
(1)
Every application under Section 14 of the Act shall be made to the APERC by any person for grant a licence.
(a)
to transmit electricity as a transmission licensee; or
(b)
to distribute electricity as a distribution licensee; or
(c)
to undertake trading in electricity as an electricity trader. In any area as may be specified in the licence, shall be
accompanied by an application fee of Rs. 30,000/-(Rupees Thirty thousand only.)
(2)
The application fee is to be paid in form of demand draft drawn in favour of 'Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory
Commission' payable at Hyderabad.
(3)
The details of demand draft shall be noted in the appropriate column of the application and also the Demand Draft must
be enclosed to the application when its is submitted to the Commission.
(4)
Application for grant of licence which is not accompanied by the application fee will not be accepted.
(5)
The licence application fee once paid will not be refunded.
