The U.P. Antarim Zila Parishad (Functioning of Antarim Zila Parishads In Newly Created Dis...
These rules shall be called the U.P. Antarim Zila Parishad (Functioning of Antarim Zila Parishad in Newly Created
Districts) Rules, 1960.
(2)
They shall come into force immediately.
2.
Whenever a new district is created all the powers, duties and functions of the Antarim Zilal Parishad, its Adhyaksh or
any Committee of the Antarim Zila Parishad having jurisdiction in any area therein immediately before the creation of the
new district shall, notwithstanding anything contained in the U.P. District Boards Act, 1922, or rules made thereunder,
vest on the Collector of such district in respect of all matters including funds and property whether under the U.P. District
Boards Act, 1922 or the Uttar Pradesh Antarim Zila Parishad Act, 1958, has been established for such district, exercise,
discharge and performe the said powers, duties and functions and shall be deemed to be the Antarim Zila Parishad for
all purposes.
