An Act to regulate the possession and transport of, and trading in mica in the Province of Bihar.
Whereas it is expedient to regulate the possession and transport of, and trading in, mica in the Province of Bihar;
It is hereby enacted as follows:-

"block mica" means any mica, other than chillas, splittings and waste mica, obtained from crude mica by any process of
trimming;

sound mica which can be obtained therefrom is less than six [square]
