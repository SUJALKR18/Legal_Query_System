U.P. Kshettra Panchayats (Distribution of Grants to Gram Sabhas) Rules, 1969
Published vide Notification No. 1125-B/33-2-245-64, dated 7th March, 1969
In exercise of the powers under Section 237 of the Uttar Pradesh [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
Adhiniyam, 1961 (U. P. Act No. 33 of 1961), read with Sub-Head (33) of Item 9 of Schedule I appended to the said
Adhiniyam, the Governor is pleased to make the following Uttar Pradesh [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]

U.P. Kshettra Panchayats (Distribution of Grants to Gram Sabhas) Rules, 1969
(Distribution of Grants to [Gram Sabhas]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
) Rules, 1969 after previous publication with Notification No. 3738-B/33-2-245-64, dated September 12, 1968. as
required under Sub-section (2) of Section 237 of the said Adhiniyam :
1.
(1)
These Rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Distribution of Grants to [Gram Sabhas]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
) Rules, 1969.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Gazette.
2.
The [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
shall decide the amount to be given as grant to [Gram Sabhas]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
for a particular work or project keeping in view the utility and genuineness of the work or project for which the grant is
being given, the public contribution raised in this behalf and the achievement of a particular [Gram Sabha]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
in the previous years.
3.
The grant shall be given to the Gram Sabhas on the basis of their performance in the preceding year in the best interest
of the public at the discretion of the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
in accordance with a resolution passed by the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
on the subject.
4.
The source from which the grant is to be given shall be specified by the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
.
5.
The purpose for which the grant is given shall be specified and it shall be made clear that the grant is to be utilised for
the specific purpose alone.
6.
The time within which the grant is to be utilised shall be specified by the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and it shall be utilised within the time so prescribed unless an extension of the time limit is approved by the [Kshettra
Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
. The grantee [Gram Sabha]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
shall move the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
concerned at least three months before the expiry of the time-limit.
7.
The terms and conditions governing the utilisation of the grant shall be specified by the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
. In case the grant is given by the Central Government or the State Government or any Head of the Department of the

U.P. Kshettra Panchayats (Distribution of Grants to Gram Sabhas) Rules, 1969
State Government or any institution, the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
shall impose on the [Gram Sabha]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
to which the grant is given, the same terms and conditions as have been imposed on it by the Central Government,
State Government, Head of the Department of the State Government or any institution as the case may be.
8.
The Khand Vikas Adhikari shall be responsible for furnishing to the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
on or before September 30 of the year following that to which the grant relates a certificate that the grant has been
utilised for the purpose for which it was granted and within the time specified therefor.
9.
No grant for a similar work or project under the same head shall be given unless the grant already given, if any, is fully
utilised to the satisfaction of the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
concerned.
10.
If a [Gram Sabha]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
fails to utilize the grant within the specified time or misutilises it by spending it for a purpose other than that for which it
was sanctioned, the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
may either ask the [Gram Sabha]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
concerned to return the grant or deduct the amount from the grant proposed to be given in the subsequent year.
11.
The [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
shall maintain a record of the distribution of grants, the refund of grants and the utilisation certificates and shall make
them available to the departmental authorities placing the grants at the disposal of the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
.
