The Rajasthan Social Education Board (Repeal) Act, 1982
No. 12 of 1982
[Received the assent of the Governor on the 20th day of September, 1982; Assent published in Rajasthan Gazette
Extraordinary part IV-A, dated 21-9-1982, page  15]
An Act to provide for the repeal of the Rajasthan Social Education Board Act, 1961.
Be it enacted by the Rajasthan State Legislature in the Thirty-Third Year of the Republic of India, as follows:-
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
This Act ma be called the Rajasthan Social Education Board (Repeal) Act, 1982.
(2)
Page 1/2
----- PAGE 2 -----
The Rajasthan Social Education Board (Repeal) Act, 1982
It shall come into force at once.
2.
Repeal of Rajasthan Act No. 22 of 1961.
- The Rajasthan Social Education Board Act, 1961 (Rajasthan Act No. 22 of 1961) is hereby repealed.
