The Administrative Tribunal (Removal of Difficulties) (Amendment) Order, 1968
Published vide Notification No. LD/EST/14-160/67-68, dated 8-2-1968
In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 17 of the G. D. D. Administrative Tribunal Act, 1965 and in partial
modification of the Government order No. LD/251/65 dated 18-6-1965, the Lt. Governor of Goa, Daman and Diu hereby
makes the following amendment to the Administrative Tribunal (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 1965, namely:-
1.
This order may be called the Administrative Tribunal (Removal of Difficulties) (Amendment) Order, 1968.
2.
It shall come into force at once.
