The Advocates/Bar Association Affiliation Rules, 1985
Published vide Notification No. Bihar Gazette, Part 2, dated 24.3.1993
Whereas the Bar Council is of the opinion that it is necessary and expedient for an unified Bar in the State and for
furthering better links with the Advocates/Bar Associations, the following rules are framed for the Affiliation of the
Advocates/Bar Association:
(a) These rules shall be called:The Advocates/Bar Association Affiliation Rules, 1985.
(b) They shall extend to the whole of Bihar.
1.
Definition.
- In these rules:
(a)

"Association" means a body of Advocates having at least 50 Members, if the Association is functioning at the District
Head quarter and in any other case having at least 25 Members provided that the Bar Council may for good and
sufficient reasons, relax the conditions relating to number of Members in respect of the Association.
(b)
"Council" means the Bar Council of Bihar.
(c)
"Advocate" means the legal practitioner whose name appears on the Roll of Advocates maintained by the State Bar
Council.
(d)
"Unit" will mean Patna and Ranchi Benches of the High Court, District, Sub-Division Income-Tax-Sales Tax and Labour
Tribunal Units at Patna, District or Sub-Divisional Head quarter.
2.
Affiliation.
- In these rules:
(a)
Every Association desirous of affiliation may apply for the affiliation on the prescribed form (Schedule I) alongwith an
affiliation fee of Rs. 1000/-for District Bar Association and Rs. 500/- for Sub-Divisional Bar Association. A renewal fee of
Rs. 500/- for District Bar Association and Rs. 250/- for Sub-Divisional Bar Association annually shall be payable by 30th
April, each year to the Bar Council for continuing the affiliation. [Fees revised w.e.f. 1.4.2005]
Provided that affiliation will be accorded at one unit to only one Association, which has the largest number of members
on the date of affiliation.
(b)
The Association shall forward to the Council the names of its members and also the names of the office-bearers every
year alongwith the application for renewal in (Schedule II).
3.
(a)
Such affiliated Association shall be under obligation to keep a vigil over the professional activities of the members of
profession and shall promptly inform the Council about any Advocate committing breach of the rules of professional
standards and etiquette or of any other misconduct which in the opinion of its Executive Committee or the General Body
is derogatory of the profession or the dereliction of his duties towards his client or fellow members and the Council may
thereupon take such action against the Advocate as deemed appropriate in the circumstances.
(b)
The Council shall keep the affiliated Association informed of every important decisions taken or resolutions passed by it
or by the Bar Council of India and the affiliated Association shall, to the best of its capacity, carry out all the directions
and instructions issued by the Council from time to time.
4.
Disaffiliation.
- The Council may disaffiliate any Association on any of the following grounds:
(a)
If any Association fails to pay the renewal fee by July 31,
(b)
If the Association deliberately or willfully disobeys or does not carry out the directions and instructions as mentioned
above,
(c)
The number of members of the Association falls below the prescribed limit,
(d)
The annual election of the Associations is not held in accordance with their rules and in spite of the request of the
Council,
(e)
If any dispute among the office-bearers of the Bar Association after election arises, the matter should be referred to the
Chairman of the Bar Council of Bihar for consideration, or
