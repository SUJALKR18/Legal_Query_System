1.
Amendment of sections 18 and 28 of Act XVI of 1908.
- In the Indian Registration Act, 1908,-
(1)
in section 18-
(i)
the word "and" after clause (e) shall be deleted; and
(ii)
after clause (e) the following shall be inserted, namely:-
"(ee) notices of pending suits or proceedings referred to in section 52 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882; and"; and
(2)
in section 28 for the brackets, letters and word "(b) and (c)", the brackets, letters and word "(b), (c) and (ee)", shall be
substituted.
