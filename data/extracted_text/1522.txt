The Import of Gold and Silver by Passengers (Form of Bill of Entry) Regulations, 1994
Short tittle and Commencement.
(1)
These regulations may be called the Import of Gold and Silver by Passengers (Form of Bill of Entry) Regulations, 1994.
(2)
They shall come into force on the 1st day of November, 1994.
2.
Form of Bill of Entry.
- The Bill of Entry to be presented for ex-bond clearance for home consumption of gold or silver from a customs bonded
warehouse by a passenger coming into India shall be in the form appended to these Regulations.
