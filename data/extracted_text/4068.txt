AN ACT FURTHER TO AMEND SRI VENKATESWARA INSTITUTE OF MEDICAL SCIENCES UNIVERSITY ACT,
1995.
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Andhra Pradesh in the Seventieth year of the Republic of India, as
follows:-
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
This Act may be called Sri Venkateswara Institute of Medical Sciences Universit(Amendment) Act, 2019.
