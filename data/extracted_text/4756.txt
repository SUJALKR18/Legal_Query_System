The Bihar Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Service Recruitment Rules, 1993
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 5, Bihar Gazette (Extra-ordinary) dated 14.1.1993)
In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to Article 309 of the Constitution of India the Governor of Bihar is
pleased to make the Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Service Recruitment Rules to regulate procedures for
Recruitment/Promotion in the Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Department, namely:
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
Page 1/17
----- PAGE 2 -----
The Bihar Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Service Recruitment Rules, 1993
This Rule may be called the Bihar Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Service Recruitment Rules, 1993.
2.
It shall come into force from the date of publication in the Bihar Gazette.
2. Definition.
- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires-
(1)
"Service" means the Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Service.
(2)
"State Government" means the State Government of Bihar.
(3)
"Rules" means Animal Husbandry and Fisheries (Fisheries) Recruitment Rules.
3. Strength of posts, classification and pay scale.
- The strength of post, their classification and pay scale of the cadre of services shall be as in columns 2 to 4 of the
Schedule.
4.
Recruitment.
- Recruitment procedures, age limit, qualification and other concerned matters of service shall be as in columns 5 to 11
of the Schedule.
5.
Disqualification for appointment.
(1)
No person (a) who has entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, or (b) who having
spouse living, has entered into or contracted a marriage with any person,shall not be eligible for appointment to the
service :
Provided that the State Government may, if satisfied that such marriage is permissible under the personal law applicable
to such person and the other party to the marriage and there are other grounds for so doing exempt any person from the
operation of this rule.
(2)
A person who is a convict or mentally unsound or declared bankrupt shall not be eligible for appointment to the service.
6.
Power of relaxation.
- The State Government may relax any of the provision of this rule.
Reservation. - The reservation policy of the State Government issued from time to time for Scheduled Castes,
Scheduled Tribes, Backward Communities and others shall be followed in case of appointment and promotion to the
service.
