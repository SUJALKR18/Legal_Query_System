The U.P. Excise (Manufacture, Import, Export, Transport, Possession and Vend of Foreign Liquor) Rules, 1996
Published Vide Notification No. 2474-E-2/13-96-196-95, dated 29th August, 1966, published in U.P. Gazette
(Extraordinary), Part 4, Section (Kha), dated 29th August, 1996
In exercise of the powers under sub-section (1) and clauses (d) and (e) of sub-section (2) of Section 40 of the U.P.
Excise Act, 1910 (Act No. IV of 1910), the Governor is pleased to make the following rules :
1.
Short title and commencement.

(1)
These rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh Excise (Manufacture, Import, Export, Transport, Possession and Vend of
Foreign Liquor) Rules, 1996.
2.
Special provision with respect to India-made foreign liquor.
- The manufacturers, wholesalers and retail vendors of Indian-made foreign liquor holding licence may bottle, import,
export, transport, posesses or vend any Indian-made foreign liquor in bottles having capacity of 60 millilitre or 100
millilitre; or any beer in aluminum containers having capacity of 500 millilitre.
