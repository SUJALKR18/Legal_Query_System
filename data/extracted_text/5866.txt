The Garo Hills Fisheries Act, 1953
(Act
No. 1 of 1953
)
Last Updated 19th February, 2020
An Act to regulate fisheries in the Garo Hills Autonomous District
Whereas it is expedient to establish and regulate the management of fisheries in the Garo Hills Autonomous District ;
It is hereby enacted as follows :
1.
(i)
This Act may be called the Garo Hills Fisheries Act, 1953.

The Garo Hills Fisheries Act, 1953
(ii)
It extends to the whole of Garo Hills Autonomous District.
(iii)
It shall come into force at once.
2.
In this Act,-
(1)
The word "Fisheries" shall have the same meaning as interpreted in the Indian Fisheries Act, 1897 ;
(2)
"Chief Executive Member" means the Chief Executive Member of the Executive Committee of the Garo Hills District
Council.
3.
(1)
All laws for the time being in force in State of Assam as may be amended and modified from time to time by the District
Council with the previous approval of the Governor of Assam, shall mutatis mutandis apply in respect of the
establishment and management of fisheries in the Garo Hills Autonomous District.
(2)
All references in the said law to Commissioner, Deputy Commissioner or the Sub-divisional Officer shall be construed as
references to the Chief Executive Member of the Garo Hills District Council.
