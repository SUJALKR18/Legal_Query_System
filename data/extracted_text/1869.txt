The Sugarcane Cess (Validation) Repeal Act, 2001
ACT NO. 35 OF 2001
[3rd September, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Sugarcane Cess (Validation) Act, 1961.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Sugarcane Cess (Validation) Repeal Act, 2001.
2.
Repeal of Act 38 of 1961.
-The Sugarcane Cess (Validation) Act, 1961 is hereby repealed.
