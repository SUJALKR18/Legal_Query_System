The Countess Of Dufferin's Fund (Repeal) Act, 2002 Act
NO. 65 OF 2002
1157.
[18th December, 2002]
An Act to repeal the Countess of Dufferin's Fund Act, 1957.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-third Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Countess of Dufferin's Fund (Repeal) Act, 2002.
2.
Repeal of Act 63 of 1957.
