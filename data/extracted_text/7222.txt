U.P. Zila Panchayat (Supervision of The Activities of Gram Panchayats and Kshettra Panchay...
the Governor of Uttar Pradesh is pleased to make the following rules relating to the supervision of the activities of the
[Gram Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
by the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
, after previous publication with Notification No. 2080-A/33-4-R-62, dated September 4, 1962 :
1.
(1)
These rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Supervision of the Activities of [Gram Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
) Rules, 1962.
(2)
They shall come into force from the date of their publication in the Uttar Pradesh Gazette.
2.
The [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
of a district shall, subject to the provisions contained in the U. P. [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
Adhiniyam, 1961, supervise the activities of [Gram Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
of the district in the manner provided hereinafter.
3.
(1)
The Adhyaksha, the Up-Adhyaksha, the Mukhya Adhikari, any officer of the district appointed ex officio as an officer of
the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
under Section 39, or any other officer of the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
duly authorised by the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
in this behalf (hereinafter called the "Inspecting Officer"), may in respect of any scheme, project, institution or other work
of a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or in respect of such of its programme as have a direct bearing on its development activities enter and inspect:-
(a)
any immovable property or work in progress under the control of a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or its officer; or
(b)
any institution maintained by, or under the control of a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]

and any records, registers or other documents kept in such institution; or
(c)
the office of [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and any records, registers or other documents maintained or kept therein.
(2)
The Pramukh of the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and all its officers and servants shall afford to the Inspecting Officer all such facilities as may be necessary to enable him
to exercise his power and discharge his duties under sub-rule (1).
4.
The Inspecting Officer may in accordance with the recommendations of the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or any of its Committees-
(a)
make suggestions to a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
to make provision for and to execute or provide any public work or amenity, or service within the scope of any scheme of
the [Kshettra Panchayat.]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
;
(b)
call for any record, register or other documents in the possession or under the control of a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or its Khand Vikas Adhikari;
(c)
require a Khand Vikas Adhikari to furnish any return, plan, estimate, statement, account of statistics, or to furnish any
information report on any matter connected with the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
;
(d)
record in writing for the consideration of a [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or its Khand Vikas Adhikari any observations in regard to its or his work or duties.
5.
Where a [Kshettra  Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
unites with  any other [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
or other local authority to carry out any work or undertaking which may benefit all the areas controlled or both or all of
them, the Inspecting Officer shall exercise the powers and perform the duties specified in Rules 3 and 4 in respect of
that work or undertaking with a view to watch the interest of the [Kshettra Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
,
6.
The provisions contained in Rules 3, 4 and 5 shall mutatis mutandis apply to the supervision over [Gram Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
.
