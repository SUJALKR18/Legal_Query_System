In exercise of the powers under clause (a) of sub-section (2) of Section 94 read with sub-sections (1) and (2) of Section
6 and sub-section (3) of Section 7 of the Uttar Pradesh Avas Evam Vikas Parishad Adhiniyam, 1965 (U.P. Act No. I of
1966 ), the Governor is please to make the following rules regarding Remuneration Payable to the Adhyaksh and
Allowances payable to the Members of the Board:
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh Avas Evam Vikas Parishad (Remuneration Payable to the Adhyaksh and
Allowances Payable to the Members of the Board) Rules, 1967.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the official Gazette.
2.
Definitions.
- In these rules unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context -
(a)
'Act' means the Uttar Pradesh Avas Evam Vikas Parishad Adhiniyam, 1965; and
(b)
'Board' means the Uttar Pradesh Avas Evam Vikas Parishad established under the Act.
3.
Remuneration, honorarium, leave, etc. to the Adhyaksh.
(1)
The Adhyaksh of the Board shall be entitled to -
(a)
an honorarium of Rs. 1,000 per mensem, provided that he is not in receipt of any salary or monthly honorarium from the
State Government on any other account;
(b)
travelling and daily allowance as admissible to First Class Government Officer,
(c)
leave on the basis of one month per year.
(2)
Notwithstanding anything contained in these rule, where the Adhyaksh receives an honorarium or holds office in an
honorary capacity, he shall receive such of the following facilities as the State Government may, in each case, specify in
this behalf:
(a)
An official residence, costing Rs. 40,000 approximately and furnishings (as in enclosed Schedule) not exceeding Rs.
5,000, without payment of rent. The items of furnishings shall not be replaced unless they have, been declared
unserviceable by the Housing Commissioner on the expiry of the period of normal wear and tear. The expenditures on
account of annual white washing and repairs and house tax will be paid by the Board. All other taxes and charges of
service nature shall be payable by the Adhyaksh;
Or
a residence carrying a standard rent not exceeding Rs.200 per mensem;
Or
if a residence is not provided, a compensatory allowance or Rs. 100 per mensem;
(b)
a telephone at his residence;
(c)
the use of board's motor car and such allowance fro the maintenance, and running of the car as may be fixed by the
State Government or in lieu of such allowance, the actual charges for the maintenance and running thereof, subject to
condition that such charges do not exceed Rs. 3,000 per annum;

(d)
authority to incur expenditure of a sum not exceeding Rs. 600 per annum on light refreshments to be served in the
meetings of the Board.
4.
Remuneration to Adhyaksh and Housing Commissioner.
- Notwithstanding anything contained in these rules or in the rules regarding condition of service of the Housing
Commissioner, the Housing Commissioner appoint as Adhyaksh in addition to his own duties shall receive such of the
facilities mentioned in sub-rules (1) and (2) of Rule 3 as the State Government may, in each case specify in this behalf.
5.
Allowances to members of the Board.
- The members of the Board shall receive travelling and daily allowances for their journeys in connection with the
meeting of the Board as admissible to Government Officers of the first class, provided that the members of the Boards
who are also members but of the Vidhan Sabha or Vidhan Parishad, shall not draw any railway fare but shall draw daily
allowance and incidental charges only when they are not entitled to draw the same from Government.
