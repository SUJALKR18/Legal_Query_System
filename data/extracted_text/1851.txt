The Vaccination (Repeal) Act, 2001
ACT NO. 16 OF 2001
1177.
[3rd August, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Vaccination Act, 1880.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Vaccination (Repeal) Act, 2001.
2.
Repeal of Act 13 of 1880.
