The Sugar (Price Determination for 2009-2010 Production) Order, 2010
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 527(E), dated 21st June, 2010
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
(Department of Food and Public Distribution)
Order
G.S.R. 527(E)/ESS.Com/Sugar. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (3C) of Section 3 of the Essential
Commodities Act, 1955 (
10 of 1955
), the Central Government hereby makes the following order, namely :-
1.
Short title and commencement.

----- PAGE 2 -----
The Sugar (Price Determination for 2009-2010 Production) Order, 2010
(a)
This order may be called the Sugar (Price Determination for 2009-2010 Production) Order, 2010.
(b)
It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Definition.
- In the Order, unless the context otherwise requires :-
(a)
"Indian Sugar Standards Grade (ISS Grades)" means the grades represented by the standard sealed samples of sugar
in bottles issued by the Director, National Sugar Institute, Kanpur, conforming to the Standards M.-29, S.-29, L.-30,
M.-30, S.-30, L.-31, M.-31, S.-31 and SS - 31 as specified by the Central Government.
(b)
"Schedule" means a schedule annexed to this Order.
3.
Price of Sugar produced in 2009-2010 Season.
- The prices as specified in Column 3 of Schedule I or Schedule II, as the case may be, against the zones specified in
the corresponding entry in column 2, shall be the prices payable for all the Indian Sugar Standards (ISS) grades of
sugar, packed in 'A', Twill new gunny bags and produced in 2009-2010 sugar season by all vacuum pan sugar factories
specified in Schedule III which are required to be supplied to any person, Organization or State Government under
sub-clause (1) of clause 2 of the Levy Sugar Supply (Control) Order, 1979 for delivery at buyer's option in (i) railway
wagons or (ii) the buyer's carts, lorries or other means of transport at the factory gate or factory godown's:
Provided that where a sugar factory is situated at a distance exceeding five kilometers from the nearest railway station
or an out agency of the railways or from the godown's outside the factory not connected, the producer may charge
additional thirteen paise per quintal of sugar per kilometer beyond five kilometers of the distance between the sugar
factory and such railway station or the out agency of the railway in case of dispatches by rail or between the sugar
factory and such godown's in case of delivery ex-godown's.
Explanation. - In this Order:-
(a)
the prices specified in column 3 of Schedule I and Schedule II do not include the excise duty or additional excise duty in
lieu of sale tax or the special excise duty as the case may be which the producer can recover at the rate in force at the
relevant time, in addition to the prices as determined under this Order;
(b)
the prices specified in column 3 of Schedule I include transport from factory godown and loading, at buyer's option, into
railway wagons at the railway station or siding generally used by the producer and including siding or wagons hauling,
dunnage and other incidentals;
(c)
the prices specified in column 3 of Schedule II includes transport from godown's and loading of sugar, at buyer's option,
into carts, lorries or other means or transport at the factory gate/factory godown's and includes all other incidentals.
4.
The prices determined under this Order shall apply to the sugar produced in the sugar season 2009-2010 and delivered
on or after the date of commencement of sugar season, that is, the 1st day of October 2009. These are provisional
prices. Recoveries on account of excess payment made, if any, or additional payments if due, will be made on
finalization of prices.
