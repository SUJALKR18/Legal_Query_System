The Orissa Bhasha Sansthan Rules, 1995
Published vide Notification No. 5084/SC/7 - CC - 27/95 Dated 21.7.1995
1.
Short title and Commencement.
(1)
These rules shall be called the Orissa Bhasa Sansthan Rules, 1995.
(2)
These rules shall come into force with immediate effect.

3.
The Sansthan.
(1)
The Sansthan shall be an autonomous Institute and shall pursue the following objects, namely :
(a)
advancement of programmes, schemes and projects relating to promotion of languages and inter-language interaction in
general and promotion of Oriya, Sanskrit, Hindi, English, Koshali-Sambalpuri and Tribal Languages and Dialects in
particular;
(b)
advancement of programmes, schemes and projects relating to linguistic studies or Oriya language in all aspects,
including palaeographic, epigraphic, etymological, lexicological, dialectological and sociolinguistic aspects;
(c)
monitoring of schemes and programmes relating to the implementing of the Official Language Policy of the State;
(d)
advancement of schemes and programmes relating to courses of instruction and training in Oriya (a) as a language of
communication for various languages including non-Oriya persons and (b) as the Official Language in administrative
usage;
(e)
advancement of schemes and programmes relating to preparation of various language learning materials, including
audio-cassettes, unilingual bilingual/multilingual dictionaries, thesaurus and anthologies of proverbs, riddles, slanges,
phrases etc;
(f)
advancement of schemes and programmes relating to coinage and compilation of Oriya terminologies in respect of
scientific, technical, administrative, legal, academic and other specialised expressions and publication of glossaries;
(g)
formulation of recommendations for Educational Language Policy of the State and for linkage thereof with the Official
Language Policy of the State;
(h)
Co-ordination of the language-based schemes, programmes, and projects operated through various Government
Organisations, autonomous institutions, public sector bodies, audio-visual media, print-media and private sector
organisations;
(i)
editing of palm leaf manuscripts, rare and valuable texts and other classics.
(j)
promotion of translation from Oriya to other language and from other languages into Oriya;
(k)
institution of fellowships, scholarships, assignment-grants, awards and prizes etc. for promotion of the objects specified
hereinbefore; and

(2)
The Sansthan shall have a General Council and an Executive Board. The Executive Board may, from time to time,
constitute Committees with such members, experts and functionaries as considered necessary for specific purposes or
occasions. No acts or proceedings of these bodies shall be invalid merely by reason of existence of any vacancies
therein or any defects in the constitution thereof.
(3)
The meetings of the General Council shall be presided over by the Chairman and, if the Chairman is absent, by the
working Chairman. The meetings of the Executive Board shall be presided over by the working Chairman and, if the
working Chairman is absent, by the vice-Chairman. The Meetings of the Committees shall be presided over by the
Presidents of the Committees concerned.
(4)
The Sansthan may collaborate with various organisations for the purpose of implementation of its objects or for
execution of specific schemes, programmes or projects.

5.
The Director.
(1)
The Director shall be selected from among experts who (a) hold Doctorate degree in linguistics or in any language
subject related to linguistics (b) have evident experience in lexicography with publications on lexicography and (c) are
within the age of fifty five, persons who are recipients of National/State Akademi awards for literacy attainments or have
organisational experience shall be preferred.
(2)
The Search Committee for selecting the Director shall comprise the Director of the Central Institute of Indian Language
or his nominee, the President of the Orissa Sahitya Akademi and two renowned scholars having expertise in Language
of Linguistics nominated by the Government. For finalising the selection, the Search Committee shall consider (a) the
experts whose applications are received in response to the open advertisement and (b) other persons having the
requisite expertise.
(3)
The appointment shall be made for a term of three years ordinarily, expendable for a second term in exceptional cases.
The incumbent shall be allowed in his parent Organisation.

6.
Funds of the Sansthan.
(1)
The funds of the Sansthan shall consist of the following ; (a) the Foundation Fund, (b) grants received from Government
of India, State Government and other organisations, (c) donations from organisations, institutions and individuals, and
(d) other miscellaneous receipts including advertisement-charges, interest from investments, sale of publications etc.
(2)
The funds of the Sansthan shall be paid into the credit of the Sansthan in one or more accounts, in the State Bank of
India and/ or any other Nationalised Banks. Such of the funds may be invested in term deposits as may be decided from
time to time. The accounts shall be operated by the Director or any other functionary duly authorised by him.
7.
Audit.
- The accounts of the Sansthan shall be audited annually by a firm of Chartered Accountants.
8.
General Provisions.
(1)
The provisions of these rules may be amended by Government after (a) placement of the draft amendments before the
General Council in a meeting specially convened for the purpose and (b) due consideration of the recommendation of
the General Council.
(2)
Vacancies in the posts of the Sansthan shall ordinarily be filled up by deputation and the incumbents shall be allowed to
retain their lien in their parent organisations.
(3)
For attending the meetings of the Sansthan, those who are functionaries of Union or State Government of their
autonomous organisations, State Universities or Public Undertakings shall draw the Travelling and Daily Allowances
from their respective organisations. Other shall be entitled to Travelling and Daily Allowances, as admissible from time to
time from the Sansthan-
(4)
The Sansthan may be dissolved by the Government after considering the recommendation of the General Council. Upon
such dissolution, the assets of the Sansthan shall be handed over either to an organisation having similar objects to the
Government, after clearing the debts and liabilities.
(5)
The Executive Board may frame regulations from time to time, for the furtherance of the objects of the Sansthan.
