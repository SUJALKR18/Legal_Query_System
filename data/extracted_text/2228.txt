Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of Residence) Amendment Regulations, 2006
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 427(E), 17th July, 2006
Ministry of Shipping, Road Transport and Highways
(Department of Shipping)
(Ports Wing)
G.S.R. 427(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 124, read with Sub-section (1) of
Section 132 of the Major Port Trusts Act, 1963 (38 of 1963), the Central Government hereby approves the Kandla Port
Employees (Allotment of Residence) Amendment Regulations, 2006 made by the Board of Trustees of Kandla Port as
set out in the Schedule annexed to this Notification.

Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of Residence) Amendment Regulations, 2006
2.
The said Regulations shall come into from the date of publication of this Notification in the Official Gazette.
Schedule
In pursuance of the provision of the Section 28 of the Major Port Trust Act, 1963, the Board of Trustees of the Port of
Kandla hereby amends the Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of residence) Regulations, 1964;
1.
(i)
These Regulations shall be called Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of Residence) Amendment Regulations, 2006.
(ii)
These Regulations shall come into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette of the approval of the Central
Government thereto.
2.
Substitute the existing Table No. I under Regulation 6 of the Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of Residence)
Regulations, 1964 by the following table:
Table No. I
Type of Residence
Eligibility of employees to the type of residence at Kandla /
Gopalpuri drawing monthly basic pay in the range given below
Type I (F)
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 5030
Type II (E)
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 5031 to Rs. 7210
Type III (D)
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 7121 to Rs. 8520
Type IV (C)
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 8521 to Rs. 12600
Type V (B)
Officers drawing basic pay of Rs. 17501 and above failing
which all Class I & II Officers drawing basic pay from Rs.
12601 to Rs. 17500
Type VI (A)
Dy. Chairman and HODs failing which Officers basic pay above
of Rs. 17501
3.
Substitute the existing Table No. II under Regulation 6 of the Kandla Port Employees (Allotment of Residence)
Regulations, 1964 by the following table:
Table No. II
Type of Residence
Eligibility of employees to the type of residence at Vadinar
drawing monthly basic pay in the range given below
'A' Type
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 5445
'B' Type
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 5446 to Rs. 7860
'C' Type
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 7861 to Rs. 12600
'D' Type
Employees drawing basic pay upto Rs. 12601 and above
