The Indian Universities (Repeal) Bill, 2001 Act
NO. 36 OF 2001
1168.
[4th September, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Indian Universities Act, 1904.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Indian Universities (Repeal) Act, 2001.
2.
Repeal of Act 8 of 1904.
