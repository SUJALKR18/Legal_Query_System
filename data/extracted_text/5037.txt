The East Punjab War Awards (Amendment) Act, 2006
Published vide Notification No. G.S.R. 699(E), dated 29th September, 2008
Ministry of Home Affairs
G.S.R. 699(E). - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 87 of the Punjab Reorganization Act, 1966 (31 of 1966),
the Central Government hereby extends to the Union Territory of Chandigarh, the East Punjab War Awards
(Amendment) Act, 2006 (15 of 2006), as in force in the State of Punjab on the date of publication of this notification,
subject to the following modifications, namely :-
1.
Modifications.
