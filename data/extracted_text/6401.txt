Form of Certificate Rules
Published vide Notification No. G. O. Ms. No. 2321, Revenue, dated 2nd June 1961 - SRO No. A-652 of 1961
Original rules published in Part V of the Fort St. George Gazette, dated 21st June 1961 (page 669).
G. O. Ms. No. 2321, Revenue, dated 2nd June 1961 - SRO No. A-652 Of 1961. - In exercise of the powers conferred by
clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 101 of the Tamil Nadu Hindu Religious and Charitable Endowments Act, 1959
(Tamil Nadu Act 22 of 1959), the Governor of Tamil Nadu hereby makes the following Rides, namely:-
1.
These Rules may be called the Form of Certificate Rules.
2.
The certificate referred to in clause (b) of sub-section (1) of section 101 shall be in the following Form.
