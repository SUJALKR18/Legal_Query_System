The Court Fees (Bihar Amendment) Act, 2007
Bihar Act
4 of 2008
[Dated 7th January, 2008]
Published in Bihar Gazette (Extraordinary), dated 8.1.2008.
An Act to amend the Court Fees Act, 1870 in its application to the State of Bihar
Be it enacted by the Legislature of the State of Bihar in the Fifty eighth year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title, extent and commencement.
(1)
It shall be called Court Fees (Bihar Amendment) Act, 2007.
