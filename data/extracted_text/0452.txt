An Act further to amend the Code of Civil Procedure,   1908.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Seventh Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title and commencement.-
(1)
This Act may be called the Code of Civil Procedure (Amendment) Act,  1956 .
(2)
It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
16.
Savings in respect of execution of certain decrees by Collectors.-
Where, before the commencement of this Act, the execution of a decree has been transferred to the Collector under
