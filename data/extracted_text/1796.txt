The Indian Power Alcohol (Repeal) Act, 2000
ACT NO. 32 OF 2000
1173.
[25th August, 2000]
An Act to repeal the Indian Power Alcohol Act, 1948.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-first Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Indian Power Alcohol (Repeal) Act, 2000.
2.
Repeal of Act 22 of 1948.
