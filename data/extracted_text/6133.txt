The Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Members (Pension) Rules, 1977
Published vide Notification No. F.7(1) Sansad/ 77, Published in Rajasthan Gazette Part 4-C(1), Page No. 57(1 to 8)
dated 28-6-1982
In exercise of the powers conferred by clause (ff) of Sub-section (2) of section 11 read with section 4A of the Rajasthan
Legislative Assembly (Officers and Members Emoluments and Pension) Act, 1956 (Rajasthan Act 6 of 1957), the State
Government hereby makes the following rules namely:-
1.
Short title & Commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Rajasthan Legislative Assembly Members Pension Rules, 1977.
(2)

They shall be deemed to have come into force on 14th December, 1976.
2.
Application of pension.
- Any application for the grant of pension under these rules shall be made by the person entitled to pension under the
Act to the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly in form No. 1:
Provided that-
(i)
in the case of lunacy of the person entitled to receive pension the application may be made by the person appointed to
manage the estate of the lunatic under the Indian Lunacy Act, 1912.
(ii)
in the case death of the person entitled to pension under the Act, the application may be made by the person who has
obtained succession certificate in respect of the arrears of pension under the Indian Succession Act, 1925 or a
certificate under the Administrators General Act, 1963.
3.
Scrutiny of application and issue of pension payment order.
(1)
On receipt of an application for the grant of pension under these rules, the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly
shall determine, after such verification as he deems necessary, the eligibility or otherwise for the grant of pension to the
person claiming pension under the Act.
(2)
In case a person in respect of whom the pension is claimed is found eligible to the grant of the same, a pension payment
order shall be issued in favour of the applicant by the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly in Form No. II.
3A.
[ No dues Certificate.
[Added by Notification No.F.7(1) vide GLR 34, Published in Rajasthan Gazette Part IV-C (1), page 307-308 dated
7-9-1979.]
(1)
There shall be enclosed with an application for the grant of pension under rule 2, a No Dries Certificate to be obtained
from the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly.
(2)
Pending issue of a No Dues Certificate required under sub-rule (1) above, the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative
Assembly may provisionally grant an amount not exceeding 75% of the pension:
Provided that the Government dues outstanding against a member shall be recoverable from his pension but the
amount of such recovery shall not exceed 1/3 of the pension.]
4.
Mode of payment of pension.
(1)
The first payment of pension and subsequent payments of pension for the months of May and November every year
shall be subject to the production (in respect of the person entitled to pension under the Act) of a declaration in Form No.
III.
(2)
The pension payment order shall be prepared in Triplicate and the first copy shall be delivered to the applicant and the
second shall be sent tot he Public Sector Bank Branch approved by the State Government or the Treasury Officer from
which the applicant has desired to draw his pension. A copy there of shall be retained for record.
(3)
(i)
The payment of pension under these rules shall be made to the person in whose favour pension payment order is
issued either at a Treasury in the State or through the Public Sector Bank at his option, the payment of pension under
these rules through the Public Sector Bank shall be, made in accordance with scheme for payment of pension through
Public Sector Banks applicable to State pensioners as and when it is introduced.
(ii)

When payment of pension is desired at a Treasury in the State the Treasury half of the pension payment order shall be
sent to the Treasury Officer concerned by the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and the payment of pension
shall alongwith a pension bill in the form given in Form IV. The record of payment of pensioner made shall be kept on
the reverse portion of the pension payment order and entries shall be made in both the halves of the pension payment
order in token of payment having been made under the attestation of the Treasury Officer.
(4)
If the person entitled to pension (under the Act and the rules) is elected or appointed to any office or post becomes
entitled to any salary, remuneration or pension, he shall inform the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly and the
pension disbursing Bank within one month of such election, appointment or entitlement, as the case may be.
(5)
A Register of all the pension payment orders shall be maintained by the Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly
recording the particulars of pension payment order issued, cancelled or revised.
5.
Issue of revised pension payment order.
- The Secretary Rajasthan Legislative Assembly on being satisfied that the revision of the Pension Payment Order
issued under rule 3 is necessary, may suo moto or otherwise, issue Revised Pension Payment Order from time to time
as to confirm to the provisions of the Act and rules and upon the issue of a Revised Pension Payment Order the
previous Pension Payment order in force shall cease to be effective:
Provided that the amount of pension shall not be reduced or cancelled without issuing a show cause notice and
affording an opportunity to the person concerned for making his representation within a time to be fixed by the
Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly.
6.
Cessation of pension on death.
- On the death of the person entitled to pension under the Act, the payment of pension shall cease forthwith and the
Pension Payment Order in force for the time being, shall be returned by the person in possession thereof to the
Secretary, Rajasthan Legislative Assembly with a declaration of death of the person who was entitled to pension under
the Act.
