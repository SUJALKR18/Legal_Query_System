U.P. Consolidation of Holdings Rules, 1954
(Rules framed under Section 45(3) of the U.P. Consolidation of Holdings Act, 1953 as amended upto date)
Chapter I
1.
Short title.
- These Rules shall be called the Uttar Pradesh Consolidation of Holdings Rules, 1954.
2.
Extent and commencement.
- They shall come into force in the areas in which, and from the date on which, the Uttar Pradesh Consolidation of
Holdings Act, 1953, other than Section 1, is made applicable.
