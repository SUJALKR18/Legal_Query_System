The M.P. Councillors (Non-Payment of Tax) Rules, 1963
Published vide Notification No. 67-U-18, dated 29th April, 1963, M.P. Rajpatra, Part 2, dated 24-5-1963
In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) of Section 433 read with sub-section (3) of Section 26 of the
Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956 (
No. 23 of 1956
), and in supersession of all rules previously made on the subject, the State Government hereby makes the following
rules, the same having been previously published as required by sub-section (1) of Section 133 of the said act, namely-
1.
Short title.
- These rules may be called The Madhya Pradesh Councillors (Non-payment of Tax) Rules, 1963.

Definitions.
- In these rules unless the context otherwise requires-
(a)
"Act" means the Madhya Pradesh Municipal Corporation Act, 1956.
(b)
"Corporation" means the Municipal Corporation of a city in Madhya Pradesh.
(c)
"Councillor" includes the Mayor and Deputy Mayor.

Information to the Government and publication of names.
- If any Councillor fails to pay the arrears of any tax due to him to the Corporation within the period specified in
sub-section (2) of Section 26 of the Act, and this office becomes vacant, the Municipal Corporation shall intimate the
State Government of the fact and the State Government shall publish the name of such Councillor in the Gazette.
