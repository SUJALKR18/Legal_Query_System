The Uttarakhand State Commission for Other Backward Classes (Amendment) Act, 2016
(Act
No. 5 of 2016
)
[Dated 31.3.2016.]
An Act further to amend the Uttarakhand State Commission for Other Backward Classes Act, 2003
Be it enacted by the Uttarakhand State Assembly in the Sixty-seventh year of the Republic of India as follows -
1.
Short title and commencement.
Page 1/2
----- PAGE 2 -----
The Uttarakhand State Commission for Other Backward Classes (Amendment) Act, 2016
(1)
This Act may be called the Uttarakhand State Commission for Other Backward Classes (Amendment) Act, 2016.
(2)
It extends to the whole State of Uttarakhand.
(3)
It shall come into force at once.
2.
Amendment of Section 3.
- in the Uttarakhand State Commission for Other Backward Classes Act, 2003 (hereinafter referred to as the principal
Act), in sub-section (3) of Section 3 in place of the words "Two Vice-chairmen" the word "Three Vice-Chairmen" and in
place of the words "Eleven Member" the words "Fifteen Member" shall be substituted.
3.
Amendment of Sections 4, 6, 16, 17 and 19.
- In Sections 4, 6, 16, 17 and 19 of the principal Act, for the word "Two Vice-Chairmen" the words, "Three
Vice-chairmen" and the place of the words "Eleven Member" the word "Fifteen Member" shall be substituted.
4.
Amendment of Section 8.
- In Section 8 of the principal Act, the following sub-sections shall be substituted in place of sub-section (5) respectively-
"(5) If the offices of the Chairman and the all three Vice-Chairmen fall vacant, the duties of the office of the Chairman
shall be discharged by such member, as the State Government may, by order direct.
