The Indian Railway Companies (Repeal) Act, 2001
ACT NO. 24 OF 2001
1182.
[29th August, 2001]
An Act to repeal the Indian Railway Companies Act, 1895.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-second Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Indian Railway Companies (Repeal) Act, 2001.
2.
Repeal of Act 10 of 1895.
