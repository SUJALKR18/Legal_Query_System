150 Years of Kuka Movement Rules, 2012
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the coinage of One Hundred Rupees and Five Rupees cOins to commemorate the occasion
of "150 Years of Kuka Movement" Rules, 2012.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
The coins of the following denominations shall be coined at the Mint for issue under the authority of the Central
Government to commemorate the occasion of "150 Years of Kuka Movement", namely: -
(a)
One Hundred Rupees;
(b)
Five Rupees.
3.
The coins of the denominations referred to in rule 3, shall conform to the following dimensions, designs and composition,
