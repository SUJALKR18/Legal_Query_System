The Mysore State Legislature (Delegation Of Powers) Repeal Act, 2002
ACT NO. 57 OF 2002
1159.
[17th December, 2002]
An Act to repeal the Mysore State Legislature (Delegation of Powers) Act, 1971.
BE it enacted by Parliament in the Fifty-third Year of the Republic of India as follows:-
1.
Short title.
-This Act may be called the Mysore State Legislature (Delegation of Powers) Repeal Act, 2002.

2.
Repeal of Act 23 of 1971.
