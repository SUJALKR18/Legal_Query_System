U.P. Zila Panchayats (Powers, Functions and Duties of Vittiya Paramarshdata) Rules, 1967
Published vide Notification No. 7765/33-1-106-67, dated 30th November, 1967
In exercise of the powers under Section 237 of the Uttar Pradesh [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
Adhiniyam, 1961 (U. P. Act No. 33 of 1961), read with sub-section (1) of Section 39 and sub-section (1) of Section 50 of
the said Adhiniyam, the Governor of Uttar Pradesh is pleased to make the following rules, after previous publication with

U.P. Zila Panchayats (Powers, Functions and Duties of Vittiya Paramarshdata) Rules, 1967
Notification No. U.O. 744/ 33-I, dated November 8, 1967, as required under sub-section (2) of Section 237 of the said
Adhiniyam :
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Uttar Pradesh [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
(Powers, Functions and Duties of Vittiya Paramarshdata) Rules, 1967.
(2)
They shall come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Gazette.
2.
Definitions.
- In these rules, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context,-
(i)
"Act" means the U.P. [Kshettra Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
and [Zila Panchayats]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
Adhiniyam, 1961;
(ii)
"Vitta Adhikari" means the Vitta Adhikari of the [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
appointed under Section 42 of the Act; and
(iii)
"Vittiya Paramarshdata" means the Vittiya Paramarshdata of a [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
appointed under sub-section (3-B) of Section 39 of the Act.
3.
Powers, functions and duties of Vittiya Paramarshdata.
- The Vittiya Paramarshdata shall, for so long as the Vitta Adhikari is not appointed in a [Zila Panchayat]
[Substituted by U.P. Act No. 9 of 1994.]
under the aforeskid Act, exercise the powers, perform the functions and discharge the duties of the Vitta Adhikari under
the said Act.
