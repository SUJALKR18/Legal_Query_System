The Printing of One Rupee Currency Notes Rules, 2014
These rules may be called the Printing of One Rupee Currency Notes Rules, 2014.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Printing.
- The One Rupee Currency Notes shall be printed at the Note Printing Presses for issue under the authority of the
Government of India for circulation.
