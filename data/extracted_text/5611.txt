Notification No. F. 23-49-97-XXV-4, dated 22nd October, 1997. - In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 17 read
with sub-section (5) of Section 4 of the Madhya Pradesh Rajya Anusuchit Jati Ayog Adhiniyam, 1995 (No. 25 of 1995),
the State Government hereby makes the following rules for regulating the salary and allowance payable to and other
terms and conditions of service of the Chairperson and Members (Unofficial) of the Commission for Scheduled Castes,
namely :-
1.
Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called The Madhya Pradesh State Commission for Scheduled Castes Salaries and allowances and
conditions of service of Chairperson and Members Rules, 1997.
(2)
They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
2.
Definitions.
- In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires-
(a)
"Adhiniyam" means the Madhya Pradesh Rajya Anusuchit Jati Ayog Adhiniyam, 1995 (No. 25 of 1995);
(b)
"Chairperson" means the chairperson of the Commission;
(c)
"Member" means an unofficial Member including the Chairperson;
(d)
The words and expression used herein but not defined in the Adhiniyam, shall have the same meaning assigned to them
in the Adhiniyam.
3.
Salary, Allowances and other facilities.
(1)
The Chairperson shall get such salary, allowances and other facilities as is fixed by the Government to a person having
the status of a Minister.
(2)
Members shall get such salaries, allowances and other facilities as is fixed by the Government for a person having the
status of a State Minister.
4.
Pension.
- The Chairperson and the Members, by reason of working in the Commission shall not be eligible for pension.
5.
Provident Fund.
- The Chairperson and the Members, by reason of Commission shall not be eligible for Provident Fund.
