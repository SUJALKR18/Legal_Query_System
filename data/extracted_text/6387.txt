The Tamil Nadu Coinage (Alteration of References) Act, 1960
Tamil Nadu Act
9 of 1960
Statement of Objects and Reasons. - According to section 14(3) of the Indian Coinage Act, 1906 (Central Act III of
1906), as amended by the Indian Coinage (Amendment) Act, 1955 (Central Act 31 of 1955), all reference in any
enactment to any value expressed in annas and pies shall be construed as references to that value expressed in terms
of decimal coins, that is, naya paise, the fractions of which are rounded off to the nearest naya paisa. Many of the
enactments in force in the State of Madras have already been amended altering references to annas and pies into
references to decimal coins. This Bill seeks to amend certain other enactments for the purpose of altering references to
annas and pies into reference to decimal coins. Enactments to which separate and comprehensive amendments are
under consideration and enactments which are shortly to be repealed have been excluded from the scope of this Bill.
