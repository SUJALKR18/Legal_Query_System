Short title and commencement.
(1)
These rules may be called the Madhya Pradesh Vidhan Mandal [Neta Pratipaksh]
[Substituted by Notification No. 6974-XXI-A (P.A.), dated 8-3-1983.]
(Residence) Conveyance and Travelling and Daily Allowances) Rules, 1980.
(2)
They shall be deemed to have come into force on the 19th days August, 1980.
2.
Application of rules to the [Neta Pratipaksh]
[Substituted by Notification No. 6974-XXI-A (P.A.), dated 8-3-1983.]
.
- Subject to the provisions of the Madhya Pradesh Vidhan Mandal [Neta Pratipaksh]
[Substituted by Notification No. 6974-XXI-A (P.A.), dated 8-3-1983.]
(Vetan Tatha Bhatta) Adhiniyam, 1980 (No. 8 of 1980) the Madhya Pradesh Ministers (Residence) Rules, 1964, the
Madhya Pradesh Ministers (Purchase and Maintenance of Conveyances) Rules, 1974 and the Madhya Pradesh
Ministers (Travelling and Daily Allowances) Rules, 1972 shall, so far as may be, apply in the case of the [Neta
Pratipaksh]
[Substituted by Notification No. 6974-XXI-A (P.A.), dated 8-3-1983.]
as they apply in case of a Minister.
3.
Interpretation.
- If any question arises as to the interpretation of these rules, it shall be referred to the State Government for decision.
